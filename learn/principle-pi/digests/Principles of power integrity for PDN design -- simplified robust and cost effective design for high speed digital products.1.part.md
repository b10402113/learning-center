# L1 overview

- Front matter establishes the book's identity: *Principles of Power Integrity for PDN Design—Simplified: Robust and Cost Effective Design for High Speed Digital Products* by Larry D. Smith and Eric Bogatin, published by Pearson (2017, ISBN 978-0-13-273555-1). This tells the learner the subject is power delivery network (PDN) design for high-speed digital hardware, not generic circuit theory.
- The e-book note and legal pages are boilerplate: EPUB customization guidance, trademark and warranty disclaimers, bulk/government sales contacts, and the production credits. Nothing here teaches PDN content, but the imprint and date anchor which edition the course follows.
- The "Contents at a glance" lists ten chapters that form a staged curriculum: define the PDN and its impedance goal, learn impedance and inductance fundamentals, learn to measure low impedance, then integrate MLCC capacitors, planes/cavities, return-plane signal integrity, the full PDN ecology, transient current and voltage noise, and finally a spreadsheet-based end-to-end design method.
- The detailed table of contents is the real skeleton for course planning. It reveals the recurring pedagogical arc in each chapter — "Why do we care?" motivation, essential principles, design guidance, advanced topic, then "The Bottom Line" summary — and names the concrete artifacts (FDTIM capacitor selection, Bandini Mountain, three-loop resonance calculator, spreading inductance, controlled-ESR capacitors) that later nodes must teach.
