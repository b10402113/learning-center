# L1 overview

- Closing summary of the workflow: the skills carry the state and process, while the human carries the judgment.
- Lists the human decisions: whether a recommendation fits the product, when to clear a muddied session, rewarding honest red results over convenient green ones, and picking reviewers who do not share the author's blind spots.
- Reminds that the agent is fast and tireless but has no stake in whether the build is successful, safe, or worth maintaining.
