# L1 overview

- A comedy sketch used to illustrate the genie mental model: a man negotiates three wishes with a genie who exploits every underspecified phrase.
- The first wish is a "wish audit" that previews the secret catch before a wish is locked in, immediately exposing loopholes in flying and lottery wishes.
- Wishes for money, leaping, world peace, existence without want, future sight, height, communing with the dead, and being the greatest singer each get twisted by literal or malicious interpretations.
- Later attempts add specificity — a chocolate bar, all books, happiness, a million wishes, all knowledge — but each still carries a catch, including the recurring eyeball loophole.
- A long, heavily qualified $40 billion wish with legal, tax, currency, and inflation conditions is granted as "the notebook you already owned," and the sketch notes he rushed despite no time limit.
- The final third is an Opera browser sponsor read covering privacy, a pop-up video player, the ARIA AI feature, messenger integration, ad blocking, and battery saving.
