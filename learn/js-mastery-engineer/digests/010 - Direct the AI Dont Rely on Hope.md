# L1 overview

- The mindset shift is from treating the AI as a genie that grants vague wishes to acting as its architect.
- Vague prompts and hoping for good output are the default failure mode.
- The better model treats the AI as an engine that needs clear directives and constraints.
- The developer defines context and expectations, much like a senior engineer guiding a junior.
- Clear instructions yield better, more manageable output; vague guidelines yield chaos.
- Moving from hopeful prompting to structured direction increases a developer's workplace value.
- The course asks developers to stop and think critically about each prompt.
