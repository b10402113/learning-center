# L1 overview

- Clearing and compacting are the two ways to reset context, both aimed at context rot but with different tradeoffs.
- Clearing completely wipes the conversation window and starts fresh with no retained context.
- Compacting summarizes the conversation into a smaller version while retaining key points.
- Compacting gives no chance to review the summary before it becomes the new context.
- Important information can therefore be lost or misrepresented when compacting.
- Clearing is predictable and eliminates uncertainty, making it safer in some situations.
- Clear when starting an unrelated task or when exact facts must be restated; compact for continuous work where restating is inefficient or history matters.
- The choice depends on how precise the next steps must be.
