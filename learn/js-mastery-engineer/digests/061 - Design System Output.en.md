# L1 overview

- The architect/develop design work also produces a live design system route (localhost:3000/design) that renders the whole visual language in the real app, not just the feed.
- The route shows surfaces, text lines, action states (good, danger, brand), the chosen movie-like font, and the full type scale, plus radius, spacing, and motion tokens.
- It also renders the swipe card and deck for rating, the horizontal rail, chips, buttons, form controls, loading/empty/error states, success and background toasts, and responsive overlays.
- The point is that the design language becomes the shared source future develop runs follow, keeping colors, radii, and accessibility consistent instead of being re-invented per feature.
