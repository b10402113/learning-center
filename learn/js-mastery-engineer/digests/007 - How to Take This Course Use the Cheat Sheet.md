- The course is hands-on: build alongside the instructor instead of only watching.

- Use an empty cheat sheet and fill it in with your own words to aid retention.
- Follow the course order because each module builds on the previous one, especially workflow and legacy-code modules.
- Attempt the challenges before checking solutions; the struggle is part of the learning.
