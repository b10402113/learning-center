# L1 overview

- The worked task is "Build the cart feature," contrasted as vague versus clear direction.
- Vague direction produces a large, silent line difference and decisions you never see, so you can only hope.
- Clear direction answers what the work is, what it touches, and what done means, giving one scoped job with real constraints.
- The lesson frames the developer as the architect who decides what good output even is, and the AI as a fast, tireless engine.
- The architect sets constraints and makes the real decisions instead of correcting with "No — not like that, like this."
- Hand-coding is portrayed as roughly 900 lines per hour with a judgement call behind every choice.
- The slide closes with a reset and a "Run the engine" action.
