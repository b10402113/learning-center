# L1 overview

- AI tools can speed up initial coding but fail when they lack context and misread intent.
- Root cause one, contextual limitations: the AI is trained on public code and does not know your codebase unless it is provided.
- Root cause two, planning misalignment: vague prompts let the AI make assumptions that may not match your goals.
- These failures produce chaotic cycles of prompting and output that are hard to verify.
- The course answers this with structured workflows that keep the AI's work understandable and controllable.
