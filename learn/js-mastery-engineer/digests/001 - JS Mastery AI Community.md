# L1 overview

- The welcome lesson gives two setup tasks: bookmark the curriculum index and join the course Discord.
- The curriculum index at jsmastery.com/course/agentic-engineering-course always holds the current version of every lesson and reference card.
- After joining Discord, run `/verify-access` with the same email used on the JS Mastery platform to link the account and unlock enrolled courses and roles.
- Channels are split by purpose: #verify-access, #introduction, #chat, #help, #bug-report, #feature-ideas, and #feedback.
- Introduce yourself twice — in the lesson comments and in #introduction — covering name, location, what you build, and what you want from the course.
- Lesson comments are for that specific lesson's questions so context stays attached; Discord is for real-time back and forth.
- The closing philosophy: you are not here to watch ten hours of video but to finish one module at a time and build something real.
