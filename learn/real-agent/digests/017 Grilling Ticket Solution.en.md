# L1 overview

- Wayfinder claims the grilling label and reads the glossary, the map, and every issue's dependency links before asking anything.
- The grill runs as rapid rounds of notification-scope questions, each with a recommendation the human accepts or corrects.
- Domain modeling keeps questions precise and surfaces naming collisions, so discussion question/reply are renamed away from quiz questions.
- Not-yet-implemented concepts (refund payouts) belong in not-yet-specified rather than being silently decided.
- The full decision catalog is posted as the ticket resolution and the issue is closed, keeping the session inside the smart zone.
- The parent map then shows the decision summary, and the next grilling ticket becomes available.
