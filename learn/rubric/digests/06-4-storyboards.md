# L1 overview

- Storyboards fixes video problems while the video is still a board of stills, before motion is built.
- Pin a note on an exact spot, drag cuts on the timeline to change shot length, and copy all comments to the agent.
- The agent can sketch a playable wireframe from one line, then build final motion graphics and export the MP4.
- It is a beta with a sample pitch included.
