# L1 overview

- RUBRIC is a toolset for working with AI agents, not another agent framework: agents are good at doing the work and bad at showing what happened.
- Agent output scatters across chats, folders and terminal windows; RUBRIC adds a findable, visual surface on top.
- Three parts, adoptable independently: Agentic OS (homepage plus the ARMS framework), Second Brain (visual memory), Console (home for micro apps).
- Each part is sold by the problem it removes: you stop guessing what got saved, and every app you build has somewhere to live.
