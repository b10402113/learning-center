# Digest — Module 3 - Interest Media

## Overview (L1)

- This module reframes social media as "interest media": feeds now serve content based on what you're interested in, not who you know.
- The exercise: open a feed and count how many posts come from brands/businesses vs. friends — you'll find far fewer from people you actually know.
- Platforms' algorithms guess who will want each post, so your followers no longer automatically see your content; the "social" part moved to messages, group chats and communities.
- The consequence for creators: accounts with literally zero followers can still reach thousands of people.
- Jay's own proof: RoboNuggets' first post (Aug 9, 2024) had zero followers and got ~2,000 views; a few posts later a video hit 900,000 views, mostly on TikTok (where he started, along with Instagram).
- The one rule that determines whether content gets served: whether it is useful in adding value to the viewer.
