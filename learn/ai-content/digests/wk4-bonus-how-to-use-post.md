# Digest — 🎁 Bonus: How to use /post

## Overview (L1)

- A ~2-minute walkthrough of the `/post` skill: from finished content to a calendar of scheduled posts.
- What `/post` does: takes one video or multiple posts and schedules them on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Threads, then returns a calendar of your content.
- Under the hood: your agent sends the video to Blotato (connected to your accounts), which does the posting; Blotato can post across other socials too.
- Setup: download the zip from the classroom page and paste the install prompt into your agent; sign up for Blotato, connect social accounts, copy the API key, and paste it into a secure file yourself.
- Flow: type `/post` with your video; nothing goes out until you approve the words for each platform and the cover it picked; say go and it schedules posts into your calendar; scheduled isn't live until the link opens.
- Two follow-ups: ask the agent to plan your week (e.g. 3 posts/day) so videos keep going out, and ask it to refine the `/post` skill with your changes; warm up brand-new accounts (especially TikTok) for a few weeks before connecting.
