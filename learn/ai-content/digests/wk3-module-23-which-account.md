# Digest — Module 23 - Which Account to Use

## Overview (L1)

- This lesson is the tactical decision before your first post: **which account** your content goes out from, plus the concept of **warming up** an account.
- Its through-line: the instructor recommends a separate account for business content (keeping personal life apart and turning the feed into a niche research feed), and argues warming up is simply using an account like a normal person.
- Teaches that "separate" doesn't mean "new": if you have an old, unused account, rename it, archive the old posts, and post from it from day one because it's already warmed up.
- Teaches the warm-up timeline: most guides say about a week (up to two weeks if careful), and ideally wait about four weeks on a new account before automating posting; these are second-hand guidelines, not rules TikTok or Instagram publishes.
- Teaches the bot symptom: fewer than 10 views a post likely means the app tagged you as a bot — warm up another account or start a new one.
- Teaches posting by hand first (before next week's automation) for better reach and to monitor how each piece does, and to turn the feed into a research feed of your niche's hooks, scripts and trends.
- Learner outcome: pick one account for this week's 5 pieces and post them by hand, warming up a new account first if needed.

### separate-account-recommendation

- Summary: The instructor's case for a distinct business/topic account.

### reuse-or-warm-up

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Wk3 - Content Creation/14-Module 23 - Which Account to Use/14-Module_23_-_Which_Account_to_Use.en.vtt#reuse-or-warm-up]]`
- Summary: A separate account can be an existing one.
- Key claims: A separate account doesn't necessarily mean starting a new one; if you have an old, unused account (made for yourself or a friend), just change the name, archive all the old posts and start posting from there; because that account is already warmed up you can start posting from day one.
- Learner-relevant: Removes the assumption that you must create and warm up a fresh account.

### how-to-warm-up

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Wk3 - Content Creation/14-Module 23 - Which Account to Use/14-Module_23_-_Which_Account_to_Use.en.vtt#how-to-warm-up]]`
- Summary: Defines warming up in plain terms.
- Key claims: Warming up is talked about in a complex way but isn't complex; platforms want accounts driven by a normal, actual person and are trying to avoid automated/bought accounts; so use the account like a normal person — watch content, follow people in your niche, like, engage and comment; if you keep getting fewer than 10 views a post, the app has likely tagged you as a bot, so use another account or start a new one and warm it up. The written page also recommends watching, following, liking and commenting like a normal person.
- Learner-relevant: Turns an intimidating concept into ordinary behaviour.
