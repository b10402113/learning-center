# Digest — Module 19 - Operating System

## Overview (L1)

- Zooms out from individual modules to show the whole "RoboNuggets content operating system" the course is building — the same OS the creator uses daily to drive the business and brand.
- Defines an operating system as a set of skills, software and principles for content that drives revenue/profit for a business.
- The simple view is a loop of four weeks: Week 1 content identity (the what and how), Week 2 ideation (idea sources), Week 3 creation (this week), Week 4 posting/distribution, with the agent at the centre.
- Recaps where the learner is: identity frameworks (Content Trifecta = what to make, Content Pyramid = how to make it) in Week 1, idea sourcing in Week 2, hooks and tone plus the two apps (RUBRIC Scripts, RUBRIC Editor) in Week 3, and posting next week.
- The detailed view maps every skill (left) and software (right) per step; the learner checks each step until it runs on its own.
