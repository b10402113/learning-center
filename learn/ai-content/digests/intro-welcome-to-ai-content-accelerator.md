# Digest — Welcome to AI Content Accelerator

## Overview (L1)

- This is the orientation lesson for the RoboNuggets "AI Content Accelerator Program": over 4 weeks you go "from your first post to an AI-powered pipeline that does a whole content team's work for you."
- The stated end deliverable is the "RoboNuggets Content OS" — the skills, systems and files that run that pipeline, reached by day 30.
- The course is broken into 4 weekly modules meant to be finished within 30 days; each week has a theme with hands-on lessons and ends with a milestone.
- Every milestone is shared with the community so members can see it and cheer each other on.
- A key through-line is community: "the lessons are half of what you get here. The other half is the people."
- Practical mechanics: lesson-list icons (🔳 focus for the week, ✅ milestone) and a ✅ button at top-right to mark classroom pages complete.
