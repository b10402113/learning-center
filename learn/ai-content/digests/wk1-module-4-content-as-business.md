# Digest — Module 4 - Content as Business

## Overview (L1)

- This module distinguishes two ways to use content: (A) a media business, where content itself is the business and earns from ads and sponsorships, and (B) content that drives a business, where content builds trust and leads toward a product/service.
- A practical test: can you associate a business the creator offers? If not, their content is the business.
- Examples of media businesses: MKBHD (Marques Brownlee) and MrWhosetheboss (Arun), consumer-tech creators whose income is mostly ad revenue and sponsorships.
- Examples of content driving a business: Amy the reimbursement dietitian (8,000 followers, clear offer), Gary Vaynerchuk (revenue from VaynerX/VaynerMedia and investments), Ahrefs (SEO tool whose channel funnels to the product), and Sabrina Ramonov (AI education → her tool Blotato).
- Jay's own stance: he has never taken sponsorships on YouTube/the newsletter to remain unbiased and protect trust; content lets potential clients/leads know him and the Robo Labs brand.
- Recommendation: if unsure, lean toward content that drives a business (especially if after revenue over fame); it can later expand into media-business monetization (AdSense, sponsors).
