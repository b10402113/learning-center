# Digest — 🎁 Bonus: Auto Animations with /animate

## Overview (L1)

- A bonus pointer to the optional `/animate` skill for adding animations to your videos, like the ones in the instructor's recent videos.
- Its through-line: after your cut is sequenced, `/animate` lets your agent add animations for you, built from a library of ready-made elements.
- Teaches that it is optional and more advanced, so you don't need to start here — come back once your cuts feel right.
- Explains how to get the skill (from j9 - /animate Skill in Jay's Resources, which includes an install prompt) and to turn on "Teach my /animate" in RUBRIC Editor so your comments on an animated clip teach /animate what you like.
- Links three of the instructor's own lessons on the skill as next steps, then points back to Module 22.
- Learner outcome: know where to find /animate and where to learn it, and treat it as a later step after the editing loop works.
- This lesson has no transcript; the digest is grounded in the written lesson page and its image.
