## Overview (L1)

- Opens the Week 2 ideation modules.
- States the module's purpose: the right approach to brainstorming content ideas.

- Promises a few skills and automation templates to help hit the week's milestone.
- Assigns the learner to think of one content post, make it, and actually post it by the end of Week 2.
- Previews the two creation levers to be covered: quality and volume.
