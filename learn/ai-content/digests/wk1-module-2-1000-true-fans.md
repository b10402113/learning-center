# Digest — Module 2 - 1000 True Fans

## Overview (L1)

- This module teaches the "1,000 true fans" principle to reframe who content is for: followership (e.g. 100K followers) is not always the end goal.
- It is framed as updating the learner's "firmware" before making content, so they can arrive at their own content identity by the end of the week.
- The core idea is Kevin Kelly's (former Wired editor, wrote it in 2008, still relevant): 1,000 people who each pay you ~$100/year is ~$100,000/year, roughly $8/month each.
- The math extends to business: 1,000 fans × $10/month software = $10,000 MRR, achieved organically (no ads).
- A real example (via Alex Hormozi) is "Amy, the Reimbursement Dietitian" — a small-following creator earning close to $1M/year by serving a narrow niche.
- The takeaway: find your true fans, add value, then offer services/products they'll pay for to build a durable content business; that foundation can then expand toward 100K.
