# Digest — Module 10 - Rethinking Volume

## Overview (L1)

- This lesson answers the volume question: "How much should you post?" and argues that a beginner should post as many pieces as they humanly can.
- Its through-line is the **Content Pyramid**: volume sits at the base, and you improve or try something new with every post on top of it.
- Teaches that platforms do not cap how much you post, and that the instructor's personal rule was 3 posts a day.
- Teaches that you should edit and post by hand from your phone at first, before automating anything.
- Teaches that volume forces iteration, so early on you should try big changes like a new channel or format.
- Learner outcome: commit to shipping a first batch of content and set up a concrete counting system for it.
- No transcript is attached to this lesson (video only); the digest is grounded in the written lesson page and the course folder.
