# Digest — Module 21 - Camera and Setup

## Overview (L1)

- This lesson addresses a common beginner worry — what camera and setup to use — and argues you don't need a new camera: the phone in your pocket is enough, and if you upgrade one thing it should be your **audio**.
- Its through-line: video quality is not the biggest indicator of content quality; clear sound is the real requirement for educational and trust-building content, so invest in sound before a fancy camera.
- Teaches **OBS Studio**, a free open-source app that records your screen and camera together, set up with your agent one step at a time, starting with the microphone and a test recording.
- Teaches using `/search-connectors` (in Jay's More Agentic Skills) to find a connector so your agent can drive OBS itself, including turning on the OBS WebSocket server; the linked OBS connector is not official and may change.
- Teaches configuring scenes (Screen + camera, Camera), hotkeys for switching views mid-recording (including a muted blank screen), and vertical aspect ratios for shorts.
- Learner outcome: set up what you already have with your agent and record one clean take today — the fifth piece will beat the first, and not because of the camera.

### scenes-and-hotkeys

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Wk3 - Content Creation/10-Module 21 - Camera and Setup/10-Module_21_-_Camera_and_Setup.en.vtt#scenes-and-hotkeys]]`
- Summary: The instructor's hands-on demonstration of switching scenes with hotkeys.
- Key claims: Using self-set shortcuts, e.g. Shift+Control+1 for the view where he is larger, Shift+Control+2 to hide the screen behind a white or black screen (with the mic muted, so he doesn't have to pause the video while recording); a vertical scene for shorts; good for long-form content and content-for-reps alike; the written prompt also asks the agent to build a "Screen + camera" scene and a "Camera" scene, set the canvas shape, and test that switches appear in the recording.
- Learner-relevant: Shows how to change what viewers see without stopping a recording.
