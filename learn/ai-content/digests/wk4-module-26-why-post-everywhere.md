# Digest — Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere

## Overview (L1)

- Establishes first principles for posting instead of chasing each app's ever-changing algorithm (each platform has a big team upgrading it daily).
- Core principle: platforms like YouTube/Instagram compete for **attention/watch time** not just with each other but with Netflix, Prime Video, and Spotify — so they surface videos people watch in full, and you watch fully when a video is useful. Quality therefore means useful.
- Completes the **Content Pyramid**: volume (base) → improve with every post → quality on top (the North Star, still achieved through reps).
- Breaks quality into the **Content Trifecta** (from Week 1): the biggest circle is what others find useful (drives watch time), blended with what you enjoy and what only you can offer — the sweet spot.
- Recommends posting every piece on every channel, especially shorts, even at zero followers; the author's example: reposting TikTok content to Instagram grew his IG to 80,000 followers via automation.

### apps-compete-for-attention

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere/4-Module_26_-_Why_Post_Everywhere.en.vtt#apps-compete-for-attention]]`
- Summary: Explains that platforms optimize for watch/attention time, so useful videos win.
- Key claims: YouTube competes on watch time with Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and also with Netflix, Prime Video, streaming apps, and Spotify; the YouTube team maximizes attention time by tuning its algorithm to serve videos that increase watch time; you watch a video in full if it is useful — that usefulness is what makes a quality video.
- Learner-relevant: reframes "quality" as usefulness and explains why useful content gets distributed.

### content-pyramid-third-tier

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere/4-Module_26_-_Why_Post_Everywhere.en.vtt#content-pyramid-third-tier]]`
- Summary: Completes the Content Pyramid by adding quality as the top tier.
- Key claims: first set up a way to ship a lot quickly (volume), then improve for every video posted; now that reps are done, the last piece is aiming for quality (the "p"); the approach doesn't change — the author still needs many reps, iterating every time toward the most quality videos possible.
- Learner-relevant: shows volume and quality aren't opposed — reps are the path to quality.

### content-trifecta

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere/4-Module_26_-_Why_Post_Everywhere.en.vtt#content-trifecta]]`
- Summary: Recaps the Content Trifecta as the composition of quality.
- Key claims: the biggest circle is finding stuff people find useful, because that drives attention/watch time; blend it with something you enjoy, and as much as possible something only you can offer, to reach the sweet spot of quality content for your personal brand or business.
- Learner-relevant: gives the learner a checklist for what "good" content is made of.

### post-everywhere-even-at-zero

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere/4-Module_26_-_Why_Post_Everywhere.en.vtt#post-everywhere-even-at-zero]]`
- Summary: Recommends posting across every channel even with no followers.
- Key claims: the fastest way to improve with lots of volume is testing content across every channel and repurposing it into every format; even at zero followers, post across all platforms, especially shorts — the same short can go to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Threads to compare results; early on you have freedom to post what you like and repeat until something clicks.
- Learner-relevant: lowers the barrier to posting everywhere now (zero followers is fine).

### example-tiktok-to-instagram

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere/4-Module_26_-_Why_Post_Everywhere.en.vtt#example-tiktok-to-instagram]]`

- Learner-relevant: concrete proof that automated repurposing builds audience with no extra effort.
