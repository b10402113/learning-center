# Digest — ✅ Wk1 Milestone

## Overview (L1)

- Recaps the whole of Week 1 and consolidates it into the "Content Trifecta" framework.
- Revisits 1000 true fans, selfless content, media company vs. business-driving content, entertain vs. educate, interest media, and big fish in a small pond.
- Defines the Content Trifecta as content that is useful to others, something you enjoy, and something unique to you.
- Details five angles for finding what is unique to you (industry/profession, life stage, country/language, execution/format, and personal brand).
- Sets the Week 1 milestone: declare and post your content identity, share it in the community for feedback (ideally as a poll), optionally using the provided agent prompt.

## Sections (L2)

### week-1-recap

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/2-Wk1 - Your Content Identity/9-✅ Wk1 Milestone/9-✅_Wk1_Milestone.en.vtt#week-1-recap]]`
- Summary: Reviews the key Week 1 concepts and the two ways content businesses make money.
- Key claims: your content identity doesn't have to be set in stone, but it's good to declare and think about it now; 1000 true fans means follower count doesn't determine a bigger/better business; selfless/audience-centered content is the default when starting; content businesses are either a media company (earning from sponsorships/advertising) or content that drives a business (recurring revenue — the faster path); content either entertains or educates, and education is the scaling choice; platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn and Instagram are better described as "interest media" than social media since much content comes from creators, not friends; it is best to be a big fish in a small pond and expand outward.
- Learner-relevant: The synthesis that grounds the milestone deliverable.

### content-trifecta

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/2-Wk1 - Your Content Identity/9-✅ Wk1 Milestone/9-✅_Wk1_Milestone.en.vtt#content-trifecta]]`
- Summary: Presents the Content Trifecta, a three-part framework for what to create.
- Key claims: continuously create content that hits two of the three points, ideally all three; the three are (1) useful/followership & brand-building, (2) something you enjoy or like, and (3) something unique to you; building a followership/brand is the goal and the "useful" circle is drawn with a solid line because it is the most important — if content is not useful, people won't follow; enjoyment matters because otherwise you'll burn out and switch topics (the RoboNuggets brand had run ~2 years at recording); the author keeps going on AI because he genuinely likes teaching it, took a master's on it, and has worked in the data field.
- Learner-relevant: The central reusable decision framework for content selection.

### finding-your-unique-angle

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/2-Wk1 - Your Content Identity/9-✅ Wk1 Milestone/9-✅_Wk1_Milestone.en.vtt#finding-your-unique-angle]]`
- Summary: Explains why the "unique" circle is the hardest and gives five angles for finding it.
- Key claims: without the "unique" circle you end up with something enjoyable and useful but possibly as broad as AI, where too many competitors already exist; angle 1 — industry/profession (dentist, doctor, real estate agent) mixed with a general topic like AI; angle 2 — life stage (student using AI, or a senior citizen since few older people use AI); angle 3 — country or language (e.g., teaching data engineering in Tagalog or Tamil to reach a narrower, less competitive market); angle 4 — execution/format (e.g., Tunde Anthony clips a video, places a fact/hype at the top, then tests the tool; the author's own TikTok style of pointing at the screen with an orange tint), noting this usually comes after iterating on a few pieces; angle 5 — your own brand/personality (your personality, how you speak, what you wear), the easiest but only unlocked once you have a following (Casey Neistat can post daily-life videos and get hundreds of thousands of views).
- Learner-relevant: Turns the abstract "be unique" into concrete starting points for the milestone.

### the-milestone

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/2-Wk1 - Your Content Identity/9-✅ Wk1 Milestone/9-✅_Wk1_Milestone.en.vtt#the-milestone]]`
- Summary: Sets the Week 1 deliverable and how to get feedback.
- Key claims: the course is action-focused — watching without posting yields no following; the milestone is to make a milestone post declaring your content identity and share it with the community, asking for feedback; optionally, send the provided prompt to an agent (Claude, ChatGPT, Hermes or Grok) so it interviews you and suggests where you land in the Content Trifecta; posting it as a poll lets people vote on which angle works best; after that you're ready for Week 2.
- Learner-relevant: The concrete output the learner must produce to move on.
