# Digest — Module 1 - What to Expect

## Overview (L1)

- This module (video + written page) sets expectations for a 30-day, action-oriented content bootcamp delivered in 4 weekly modules.
- The ambition stated up front: by the end, the learner has an AI-powered pipeline that does a whole content team's work.
- Jay shares the principles, toolkits and custom software he used to grow RoboNuggets from zero to teaching more than a million people a month, plus the framework creators used to scale from zero to their first 100,000 followers.
- A central framing is becoming a "net positive creator": because posting takes viewers' attention and time, you must give enough value to continuously build trust for a personal brand or business.
- The 4-week plan runs identity → ideation → creation → distribution, and the course is explicitly action-oriented: "this is not a Netflix show," the learner must create, ship and post.
- Week 1 (this week) is about content identity and updating "mental firmware," moving from consumer to producer.
