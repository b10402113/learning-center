# Digest — 🔳 This Week's Focus: Distribution

## Overview (L1)

- Opens Week 4 of the AI Content Accelerator Program: after ideation and creation in the first three weeks, this week is about **distribution** — getting the most out of every piece of content already made.
- Sets the week's agenda across three topic areas: the kinds of content and channels worth your time (and which to start with), why to post everywhere even at zero followers, and scheduling across channels with `/post` and Blotato while an AI agent does the heavy lifting.
- Frames the payoff as zooming out on the whole Content OS — from idea to post — in one view.
- States the concrete weekly outcome: ship **at least 15 posts** across your channels by the end of the week.
- Through-line: distribution multiplies content you already made, and automation lets your agent handle the repetitive posting work.
