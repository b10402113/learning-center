# Digest — ℹ️ Want to learn the technology side?

## Overview (L1)

- A short note that precedes the course: the AI Content Accelerator is built around content — finding your angle, making it, and getting it seen.
- It exists to separate the content track from the technology track: learners who want the tech side are pointed to a separate course.
- The tech-side pointer is the "Agentic AI Masterclass," which sits in the classroom and covers how AI agents work and how to build with them effectively.
- The framing offered: "You get the tech skills there, and here you get a way to show the world your expertise."
- Authored by Jay; it is a positioning/redirect lesson, not a teaching lesson.
