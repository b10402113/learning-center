# Digest — Module 24 - Welcome to Distribution

## Overview (L1)

- Welcome module for Week 4: frames **distribution** as the phase that gets the most out of every effort put into creating content.
- Recaps the program arc — Weeks 1–3 covered ideation and creation, Week 4 covers distribution.
- Previews the week: where to post (channels worth your time, and which to start with), how to post everywhere with an AI agent doing heavy lifting, and a recap of the whole pipeline ("Content OS") in one view.
- Sets the milestone: by end of week, share with the community a post that you have successfully redistributed across social media channels.
- Through-line: letting agents handle the repetitive cross-posting and scheduling so one creator can operate like a team.
