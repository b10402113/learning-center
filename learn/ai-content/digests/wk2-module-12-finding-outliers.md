# Digest — Module 12 - Finding Outliers

## Overview (L1)

- This lesson introduces the second proven idea source: **outliers**, posts that beat their own normal.
- Its through-line is that an outlier is relative, not absolute—a post only needs to beat what an account usually gets, not reach a fixed big number.
- Teaches that you can find outliers with an existing tool (vidIQ, which shows an outlier score as you browse YouTube and Instagram) or, at the start, with the `/outliers` skill that does the maths for you.
- Introduces the `/inspo` skill and Chrome extension for saving outlier videos into your own library / inspiration wall.
- Teaches that outliers surface trends for businesses too, using a viral Filipino bakery TikTok as an example.
- Learner outcome: compute outliers in your niche, shortlist the ones you can talk about, and copy them or add your own unique take.
- A `resources/` folder is attached (the `outliers` and `inspo` skills: READMEs, LICENSEs, and code trees such as `skills`, `extension`, `bridge`, `page`, `rubric-inspos`, plus `robonuggets-outliers.zip` and `robonuggets-inspo.zip`); its code is digested separately and was not read for this digest.

## Sections (L2)

### what-an-outlier-is

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/3-Wk2 - Content Ideation/7-Module 12 - Finding Outliers/7-Module_12_-_Finding_Outliers.en.vtt#what-an-outlier-is]]`
- Summary: Defines outliers and corrects the common belief that they need huge view counts.
- Key claims: An outlier is a post that beats its normal; contrary to popular belief it doesn't need a lot of views; a video with 33,000 views on a channel of ~6,000 subs that usually gets ~4,000 is almost 8x its normal, which is a clear signal the topic is interesting; the concept scales for bigger creators—for Chris Williamson, a 1M video on a 500K-usual channel is 2x and is an outlier, whereas 500K is not.
- Learner-relevant: Reframes the learner's search from "big numbers" to "relative performance," which small creators can actually hit.

### vidiq-outlier-score

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/3-Wk2 - Content Ideation/7-Module 12 - Finding Outliers/7-Module_12_-_Finding_Outliers.en.vtt#vidiq-outlier-score]]`
- Summary: Shows vidIQ as an established tool that computes outlier scores while you browse.
- Key claims: vidIQ (not a sponsor, but widely used) shows an outlier score as you browse YouTube and Instagram, e.g. a video at 2x normal, another at ~7.5x, a 12K-sub channel's video at ~13x normal with 108,000 views; the same is true for longs and Shorts; the instructor saves outliers while browsing and adds them to his own mini database.
- Learner-relevant: Gives a ready-made browsing tool for spotting winning topics at a glance.

### the-outliers-skill

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/3-Wk2 - Content Ideation/7-Module 12 - Finding Outliers/index.html#the-outliers-skill]]`
- Summary: Presents `/outliers` as the DIY alternative to paid tools.
- Key claims: You don't need vidIQ to start; `/outliers` auto-computes the outlier score given a set of videos (or lets Claude/your agent search your industry for you), and ranks the top outliers; it finds posts across YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, X, Instagram and LinkedIn; the maths is each post's views divided by the account's normal views; type `/outliers` and results open on a page you can sort and shortlist; install via the provided zip prompt.
- Learner-relevant: Makes outlier research free and repeatable for beginners.

### inspo-skill

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/3-Wk2 - Content Ideation/7-Module 12 - Finding Outliers/index.html#inspo-skill]]`
- Summary: Presents `/inspo` for saving videos to your own library in one click.
- Key claims: `/inspo` saves a YouTube video to your own library via a Chrome extension; clicking Inspo on any YouTube video lands it as a card on your inspiration wall, as a tab in your RUBRIC console or a page of its own; because the library was made with Claude, Claude can access it to find relevant items based on the day's context.
- Learner-relevant: Turns scattered browsing into a searchable personal outlier library your agent can use.

### outliers-surface-trends
