# Digest — 🔳 This Week's Focus: Identity

## Overview (L1)

- This is the Week 1 opener: the week is about "the ideas that decide every content choice after it," and about picking the identity the learner will build on.
- It previews the week's learning outcomes and positions identity as the foundation for everything later in the course.
- Learners are told they will learn what to expect from the course and why acting beats watching.
- They will understand the 1,000 true fans idea and why they don't need 100K followers.
- They will learn the core ideas behind content that works: interest media, content as a media business, educate over entertain, starting in a small pond, and selfless content.
- The week ends with declaring a content identity and testing it against those ideas; the promised outcome is understanding how top creators think about content so you can stand out from day one.
