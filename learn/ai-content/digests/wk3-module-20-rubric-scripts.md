# Digest — Module 20 - RUBRIC Scripts

## Overview (L1)

- This lesson introduces **RUBRIC Scripts**, the RoboNuggets custom software that turns your content ideas into scripts ready to record, all kept in one place instead of lost in a chat window.
- Its through-line: the ideas you gathered last week (/outliers, /latest, your own experience) are fed to one command, `/rubricscripts`, and your agent writes scripts using your `/hooks` and `/tone`, right inside a browser app that runs on your own computer.
- Teaches that RUBRIC is the set of agent-ready custom software the community builds; RUBRIC Scripts is designed to be used alongside an AI agent and driven by skills.
- Teaches the review loop: pick from 3 hooks, highlight text to leave comments like a Google Doc, then copy all comments and have your agent fix every script in one go; with "Teach my /tone" on, those comments also refine your tone skill.
- Teaches a batch recording workflow: read all your scripts off one long page and record them in one continuous take, then let the agent cut them apart in the Automated Editing lesson.
- Learner outcome: install RUBRIC Scripts, generate and review a first batch of scripts, and get them onto a teleprompter (or Telegram/computer) to record.

### interface-and-reviewing-scripts

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Wk3 - Content Creation/8-Module 20 - RUBRIC Scripts/8-Module_20_-_RUBRIC_Scripts.en.vtt#interface-and-reviewing-scripts]]`
- Summary: A tour of the RUBRIC Scripts interface and how scripts are reviewed.
- Key claims: Invoking /rubricscripts with your ideas (as detailed or sparse as you like) makes the agent give you a hosted link, usually a local host URL; the app lists your scripts, which you can search and filter, sort (custom order, last edited, newest, shortest read time, alphabetical), and view as cards or a list; light or dark mode; the "add a new script" pop-up tells you to use the rubric scripts skill; opening a script shows your ideas on the left that you can toggle through; by default it gives 3 hooks to choose from, and you can edit them manually.

### teach-my-tone-and-copy-comments

- Locator: `[[sources/ai-content/20261015/4-Wk3 - Content Creation/8-Module 20 - RUBRIC Scripts/8-Module_20_-_RUBRIC_Scripts.en.vtt#teach-my-tone-and-copy-comments]]`
- Summary: How to leave feedback on scripts and turn it into both fixed scripts and a better tone skill.
- Key claims: Once your tone and hook skills are dialed in you may not need to review every hook; to give feedback, highlight the sentence or text and a pop-up lets you comment (e.g. make this shorter); you can dictate comments with a voice tool like Aqua Voice or Wispr Flow and they collect into a rolling list; the caption is shown alongside with EEO best practices, and the caption is customizable (e.g. shorter captions, more hooks) by telling your agent to amend RUBRIC Scripts; "Copy comments for all scripts" copies the whole command to your clipboard; the "Teach my /tone" toggle also makes Claude Code amend your /tone skill so your comments get built into it; paste the copied comments to Claude and it fixes all the scripts in one go.
- Learner-relevant: The core refinement loop — comments fix scripts now and teach your tone skill for the future.
