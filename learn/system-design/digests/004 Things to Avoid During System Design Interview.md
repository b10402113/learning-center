### 1. Don’t Ignore the Requirements

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-1-dont-ignore-the-requirements]]`
- Summary: Neglecting to Clarify : Failing to ask questions or clarify requirements can lead to a design that misses the mark. Oversimplifying the Problem : Don’t oversimplify the problem or ignore the complexities involved.
- Key claims: See summary
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 2. Don’t Dive into Details Too Soon

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-2-dont-dive-into-details-too-soon]]`
- Summary: Rushing into Low-Level Details : Starting with low-level details before establishing the high-level design can make your solution seem disjointed. Losing Sight of the Big Picture : Focus first on the overall architecture and how different components interact.
- Key claims: See summary
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 3. Don’t Stick Rigidly to One Idea

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-3-dont-stick-rigidly-to-one-idea]]`
- Summary: Being Inflexible : Being too rigid with your initial idea can prevent you from considering better alternatives. Ignoring Interviewer’s Hints : The interviewer might provide hints or feedback; not adapting your design accordingly can be seen as a lack of collaboration or adaptability.
- Key claims: See summary
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 4. Don’t Overlook Trade-offs

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-4-dont-overlook-trade-offs]]`
- Summary: Ignoring Trade-offs : Every design decision has trade-offs. Not discussing these can show a lack of depth in your understanding. Failing to Justify Decisions : Be prepared to explain why you chose one approach over another.
- Key claims: See summary
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 5. Don’t Neglect Non-Functional Requirements

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-5-dont-neglect-non-functional-requirements]]`
- Summary: Overlooking Scalability, Reliability, etc. : Focusing solely on functional aspects and neglecting non-functional requirements like scalability and reliability is a common mistake. Not Considering System Constraints : Real-world constraints such as cost, time, and existing technology stack should be considered.
- Key claims: : Focusing solely on functional aspects and neglecting non-functional requirements like scalability and reliability is a common mistake
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 6. Don’t Under-Communicate

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-6-dont-under-communicate]]`
- Summary: Poor Explanation : Failing to clearly articulate your thoughts can leave interviewers unsure about your understanding and approach. Not Engaging the Interviewer : This is a dialogue, not a monologue. Engage with the interviewer, ask questions, and be receptive to feedback.
- Key claims: Not Engaging the Interviewer : This is a dialogue, not a monologue
- Learner-relevant: Core system design concept

### 7. Don’t Be Overconfident or Arrogant

- Locator: `[[sources/system-design/completed/20260825_004 Things to Avoid During System Design Interview.html#h3-7-dont-be-overconfident-or-arrogant]]`
- Summary: Overconfidence : Being overly confident can lead to dismissing valuable feedback or overlooking key aspects of the problem. Not Acknowledging What You Don’t Know : It’s okay not to know everything. Being open about areas you are unsure of is better than providing incorrect information.
- Key claims: See summary
