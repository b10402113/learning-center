# Digest — 1.1 Welcome and Your Route Through This Module

## Overview (L1)

- This is the orientation lesson for Module 1 of "AI Animations & Workflows": the learner will build a short narrated animation where the AI does most of the file work and the learner directs the result.
- The AI writes the script, makes the voice, builds the scene, runs supported tools, and assembles the finished video; all of that work lives in a reusable project folder on the learner's computer.
- The learner's job is to choose what the video should teach and to verify that the output actually explains it — diagnosing when a voice sounds wrong or a picture misses the point, and knowing what to ask the AI to change.
- The whole module walks one small shared project — the fictional note "Sam will send the draft on Friday" — through five reviewed stages: script (words), voice, visual plan (storyboard), scene, and export.
- Lesson order: watch 1.2 (a full overview of how the companion video was made) before installing anything; 1.3 then offers two starting routes — a prepared Premium workspace or one the learner builds with their AI.
- There is no installation task in this welcome lesson; the only exercise is to write one sentence about something the learner wants to explain and who needs to understand it, to be reused in 1.8.
