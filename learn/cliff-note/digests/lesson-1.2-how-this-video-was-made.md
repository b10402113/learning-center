# Digest — 1.2 How This Video Was Made

## Overview (L1)

- Lesson 1.2 is an orientation tour: a roughly seven-minute demo video (`video.mp4`) that shows the finished product before the learner builds anything. No download or install is needed to watch it.
- The demo decomposes video production into **five sequential stages**: voice → timed words → storyboard → scene (animation) → render. Each stage is framed as a handoff with a check.
- The organizing metaphor is a film studio: the lesson walks the "studio analogy" (why split work into stages) and maps a vintage film pipeline onto the AI pipeline (frames show "THE STUDIO", "THE LEICA REEL", "THE SAME PIPELINE", "THE SWEATBOX", "OUR EXPOSURE SHEET").
- A core teaching move: at every handoff, name **what the AI reads, what file it writes, and what a human checks** before continuing. The lesson asks the learner to do this for one stage ("input / output / check").
- The video is narrated by a character (captioned as "DOUG" in every frame) and alternates illustrated scenes with a deliberate human/AI division of labour ("Claude does the drawing, and I'm…").
- Lesson 1.3 continues by getting a workspace ready (Premium Vault workspace or a self-built smaller version), aiming at the same "Sam/Friday" example, voice first.
