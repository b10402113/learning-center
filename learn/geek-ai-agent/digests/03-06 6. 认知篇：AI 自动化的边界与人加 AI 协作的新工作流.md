# Digest — 03-06 6. 认知篇：AI 自动化的边界与人加 AI 协作的新工作流

## Overview (L1)

- Hook — Bun merged ~1M lines of Rust in 6 days (99.8% tests passing), mostly via Claude Code. The numbers suggest full autonomy, but the truth is the opposite: Sumner designed a tight human+AI workflow, and the numbers are a ceiling, not a baseline.
- Workflow changed — Not just "AI writes code": the whole software-engineering chain is redefined across three dimensions — deliverables (产出物), collaboration, and feedback loops.
- Automation boundary — Real production data tops out around 30% dev-time reduction; autonomous coding is still demo-stage; reliability, not capability, is the bottleneck; pure automation is often more expensive.
- Enterprise failure — "Full-flow AI" projects fail because a system problem is treated as a tool problem; enterprise AI knowledge bases fail from four multiplied gaps (documentation coverage, quality, retrieval, user ability).
- The real form — The genuinely scarce, AI-era skill is designing a human+AI collaborative workflow with clear deliverables, roles, and feedback loops at every stage.

### 二、工作流变了什么：产出物、协作、反馈

### 三、"AI 自动写大软件"的真相
- Locator: `[[sources/geek-ai-agent/20260922/03-06 6. 认知篇：AI 自动化的边界与人加 AI 协作的新工作流.html#AI自动写大软件的真相]]`
- Summary: Optimist and skeptic evidence combined.
- Key claims: optimists — Claude Code 4% of commits, 10–30 PRs/engineer/day, Spotify no hand-written code since Dec, 14.5h task span, C compiler in ~2000 sessions, Bun; pure-AI-suitable scenes share clear scope, clear success criteria, reference implementation; skeptics — Anthropic's own incidents (deleted remote git branch, uploaded auth token, production DB migration), GitHub Issue #42796 (agents degrading to "lazy and wrong"), arXiv 3800+ issues, SitePoint failure modes (infinite correction loops, hallucinated paths, destructive shared-module edits); synthesis — can't build a large new system from scratch autonomously; can do bounded mid-to-large tasks; the bottleneck is reliability, not ability.
- Learner-relevant: Gives the learner a calibrated, evidence-based expectation of AI autonomy.

### 五、"企业全流程 AI 化"的真相
- Locator: `[[sources/geek-ai-agent/20260922/03-06 6. 认知篇：AI 自动化的边界与人加 AI 协作的新工作流.html#企业全流程AI化的真相]]`
- Summary: Enterprise-scale automation is even further from reality.
- Key claims: McKinsey 4-level model, but most firms are Level 2 and even the Level 4 vision is "AI runs at night, humans judge by day"; Bain — coding adopted earliest but savings insignificant, most stuck at pilot; IBM — generative AI is non-deterministic, so results require heavily human-designed "program constraints" (essentially SDD/Harness); root failure is treating a system problem as a tool problem (no information flow across stages); enterprise AI knowledge bases fail from four multiplied gaps (coverage <50%, quality, retrieval, user ability) yielding ~24% real effect; consensus: cut "full-flow" ambition, focus 1–2 real pain points, measure real metrics, accept a 5–10 year horizon.
- Learner-relevant: Prepares the learner to avoid enterprise over-promising and misdiagnosis.

### 六、看清边界：30% 提速是顶级水平
- Locator: `[[sources/geek-ai-agent/20260922/03-06 6. 认知篇：AI 自动化的边界与人加 AI 协作的新工作流.html#看清边界30%提速是顶级水平]]`
- Summary: Shared truths across single-point and process automation.
- Key claims: automation is severely overhyped (10x talk vs 30% data); consultants sell vision, product vendors sell tools, neither sells reality — filter to neutral sources; successful "AI auto" cases share clear scope, verifiability, limited context, not-from-zero, reference implementation; all big-software/full-flow work needs human+AI collaboration, not full autonomy; reliability is the real bottleneck (enterprise needs several nines); strict constraints trade flexibility for stability; pure automation often costs more tokens than human+AI.
- Learner-relevant: A reusable checklist for judging any "autonomous AI" claim.

### 七–八、真正的人加 AI 协作新工作流 + 为什么以前没人能做到
- Locator: `[[sources/geek-ai-agent/20260922/03-06 6. 认知篇：AI 自动化的边界与人加 AI 协作的新工作流.html#人加AI协作新工作流]]`
- Summary: The synthesis and the historical reason this capability is newly accessible.
- Key claims: don't stay in the old "AI writes code" cognition (caps at 1.5x); don't be fooled by "full autonomy" (model upgrades can't supply business judgement, team constraints, product direction, sign-off); train the middle — design a human+AI workflow with clear deliverables/roles/feedback; this is neither tool fluency nor prompt tricks, both of which expire; previously this workflow-design ability was embedded in organizations/roles (PM, architect, dev, test, ops) and only rare "full-stack/super individuals" had it; AI lowers the skill floor (you need to judge AI's 80% draft, not produce it yourself) but not the judgement floor, so it must be trained in real projects.
- Learner-relevant: The course's central training objective and its rationale.
