# L1 overview

- Jay's Wk3 opening lesson: before outreach tactics, fix the mindset — most new starters stall emotionally (fear of rejection, looking stupid, being salesy), not from lack of knowledge.
- Core reframe: "everything is just math" — a 1% reply rate on 100 DMs is data, not failure; rejection is depersonalized and tunable.
- Track input KPIs (messages sent) not output KPIs (clients closed), because inputs are fully in your control; work backward from close rates to required volume.
- Goal this week is conversations, not closing; spam is blasting irrelevant messages, while targeted outreach that leads with value is a service.

# L2 detail

- `#L1-L59` — Week framing: technical skills and offer are in place, now it's outreach week; the module covers the success mindset, seeing outreach as math instead of rejection, and mastering warm and cold outreach.
- `#L60-L120` — Why mindset comes before tactics: tactics without the right headspace go unused; the blockers are emotional (fear of rejection, looking stupid, feeling salesy); peers take perfect notes and never act, so knowledge wasn't the problem — headspace was; the fix is the realization that everything is just math.
- `#L121-L249` — The math reframe worked through with numbers: 100 DMs and 1 reply = a 1% response rate you can tune (message, targeting); 10 conversations and 1 close = 10% close rate, so 5 clients needs roughly 50 conversations, which is a more manageable problem; "a" no-reply is one data point, not a verdict; 1% daily improvement compounds to ~37x over a year, so consistency beats early perfection.
- `#L250-L338` — Input KPIs vs output KPIs: detach from outcomes like "3 clients this week" because they depend on other people and circumstances; instead track what you control, e.g. 100 messages this week or 10 DMs a day; example — 50 messages produced 3 conversations, so to reach 5+ conversations you simply double the inputs; reps are unambiguous actions with no waiting on others.
- `#L339-L514` — The spam worry: reaching out to personal contacts may feel weird, but intention makes the difference — trust the skill you built is genuinely helpful; spam is blasting irrelevant messages to people who don't care; even cold outreach can add value (an audit, a design mockup, an unrequested but welcome insight) so messaging at scale is okay when it leads with something useful.
- `#L515-L605` — This week's real goal is conversations, not closing: take the pressure off, no salesperson persona needed yet, contracts/invoices/delivery structure come later; closing summary — it's all math, rejection is data, track inputs, conversations are the goal; doing just this puts you ahead of the 90% who never start; next module: warm outreach, the fastest path to the first client.
