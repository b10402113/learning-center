# L1 overview

- Jay opens the 8-week Agents-as-a-Service bootcamp: go from AI hobbyist to independent provider with a real delivery pipeline, a first paying client for beginners, and a path to at least 10K MRR in 60 days.
- Sets three mindset principles: the Transition Curve (expect the valley of despair), reps over perfection (volume beats planning; Jay grew RoboNuggets from zero to 400K+ followers), and clarity & accountability (make the dream outcome visible, share it, find an accountability partner).
- Positions the community as "university" — milestones are designed to be shared, and weekly milestones are the most important part of each week.

# L2 detail

- `#L1-L118` — Welcome and framing: an 8-week bootcamp taking a daily AI user (ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Antigravity, agents) to earning from it. Outcome: first paying client for beginners, at minimum a real delivery pipeline and an established AI practice; in 60 days become an independent provider managing real clients with a clear path to at least 10K MRR. Existing practitioners benefit too — new tools, frameworks, AI-native thinking, offering AI solutions to existing clients.
- `#L119-L364` — Why now: every industry, vertical, and sector with intelligence work will be transformed by AI. Course plan: 8 weeks of modules designed to complete "while living your life," but the most important piece is the weekly milestone — one clear actionable action per week. Watching without acting "is not going to work." Milestones also drive community interaction; treat the course and community like university — the network is where the extra value is, and connections open doors for the practice.
- `#L365-L604` — Principle 1, the Transition Curve (a.k.a. emotional cycle of change, developed by psychologists in the 1970s): four stages — uninformed optimism (excited, everything possible), informed pessimism (harder than expected, doubt), valley of despair (stuck, overwhelmed, ready to quit), informed optimism (things click, real skills, visible progress). In AI this is sharpened by new models/tools every month; knowing the valley is normal is the point, and "the only way out is through." AI is a transformative technology not to be undersold.
- `#L605-L828` — Principle 2, reps over perfection: the fastest way through the valley is volume of actions/shots; posting on socials is free so volume is a lever. Jay's proof: RoboNuggets went from zero followers to 400K+ across socials and newsletter; in AI since a data science masters ~6 years ago but only started sharing publicly in 2024; early TikTok content was "as low effort as it gets" (filming his screen with a phone, using a pointer as a visual hook); posting 2–3 times per day built 180K TikTok followers and million-view videos. First client attempts and outreach DMs are muscle-building — by the 100th repetition you are multitudes better than at the start.
- `#L829-L1020` — Principle 3, clarity and accountability: be clear on what you want and build systems to be held to it. Common goals: quit the corporate job, earn USD 10,000/month (location-dependent), work from anywhere, build something you own. Make the goal visible (phone wallpaper, pinned note) and set a target date for drafting later steps. Optional but powerful: share a goal statement in the community using the template/prompt below the module — declaring goals increases follow-through via social pressure ("good pressure"). Take it further by finding an accountability partner at a similar stage.
- `#L1021-L1181` — Jay's background to establish credibility: over a decade building technical solutions, data automations, and marketing campaigns for known brands; masters in data science at University of Technology, Sydney (the "big data" era); now runs RoboLabs (AI solutions practice) and RoboNuggets (education arm, one of the largest AI communities globally); 400,000+ followers built with the same AI/automation tools taught in the course. Bottom line: "what we're teaching here is not just theory — what actually worked and continues to work." Teaser: next module explains why this opportunity is real and why now.
