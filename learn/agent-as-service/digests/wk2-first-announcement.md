# L1 overview

- Jay's module on making your first public announcement that you've started an AI practice: LinkedIn post templates plus a strategy for seeding referrals in everyday conversations. Announcing is framed as information dissemination, not bragging — you don't need to be slick, just honest.
- Three reasons to announce: people can't refer you if they don't know you exist, your network genuinely wants to help, and AI services are among the most in-demand skills right now. LinkedIn is the natural venue; templates (simple / story / casual) can be adapted with any AI model.
- Conversations are "sometimes even more powerful" than posts: every chat is a chance to plant the seed. Key stat cited: per Harvard Business Review, 84% of B2B decisions start with a referral; referrals convert better than cold outreach, which is "brutal." Minimum for the module: one LinkedIn post and some started conversations.

# L2 detail

- `#L1-L112` — Framing and why announce: with a name, website, and email done, it's time to tell people; announcing feels awkward but "this is how opportunities start," and you don't need to be slick ("CLC"), just honest. Reason 1: people can't know — you can't find clients unless you tell them; reason 2: your network wants to help sincerely (friends, former colleagues, LinkedIn connections may know someone who needs exactly what you offer, but they can't refer you if they don't know you've started selling AI solutions); an announcement is information dissemination, not bragging.
- `#L113-L236` — Continued rationale and LinkedIn choice: AI agents/AI solutions is one of the most in-demand skills and services in the world right now, so being seen as legitimate — and as reliable and sincerely passionate — helps you get your first client. LinkedIn is the natural place because it's the one social platform where professional conversations happen. Send the templates below to your AI model of choice ("LGBT" [transcription artifact for a model], Claude, or Gemini) and adapt. Template 1: a simple announcement — personal news, you started an AI practice, here's the website. Template 2: story-driven — what you've done the past few months, your decision point to turn it into a proper AI solutions agency. Template 3: casual — trying something new, offering AI agents as a service, hoping someone in your network reaches out. Pick the one that feels most like you, customize, hit post.
- `#L237-L388` — Conversations as announcements: mentioning your practice in conversation is powerful, sometimes more so than a post; every conversation (dinner with friends, a party, catching up with an old friend) is an opportunity to plant the idea. Jay's own story: when establishing his AI solutions practice he casually brought it up among peers and colleagues; because AI is so big and everyone wants to know how it can help their business, they're naturally drawn in — and the difference now is you have a website and a practice to back you up instead of just talking about tools like Claude Code, so you can steer toward potential clients they might know.
- `#L389-L484` — Side-hustle framing and the referral case: if you still have a corporate job, be honest about it and frame it as a side practice — verbatim-worthy example script: "I've actually started a little practice on the side. Basically, I'm helping businesses with AI automation and AI agent implementation. So it's just something that I'm exploring for now, but it's been really interesting." Referrals compound: the more people who know, the more likely someone thinks of you when an opportunity comes up. Share without asking for anything — you're just sharing what you're up to, now backed by the foundational steps from previous modules.
- `#L485-L560` — The stat and referral economics: per Harvard Business Review, 84% — almost 9 in 10 — B2B decisions start with a referral, not a salesperson or a Facebook/Meta ad, but a recommendation from someone they trust. Your first clients will come from your immediate network. Cold outreach can produce clients (future module topic) but is "brutal"; conversion data between referrals and cold emails clearly favors referrals. Bottom line: in every casual conversation, professional or otherwise, mention you've started this AI practice to plant the seed that could lead to your best client; referrals aren't nice-to-have, they're how B2B services actually work.
- `#L561-L629` — Community angle and module minimum: several members of the group have connected and found clients from within the community, which is why Jay encourages sharing wins, learnings, and the course milestones' required actions to build connections and trust. Minimum deliverables for this module: do one LinkedIn post and start some conversations — "you might be surprised at some of the doors that that will open."
