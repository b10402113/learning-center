# L1 overview

- Jay (RoboNuggets) sets the Wk7 mindset: build inbound through content instead of grinding cold DMs — content is a compounding asset, AI is the most talked-about space, and content builds trust before the first conversation.
- Proof points: Robolabs.so advertises anchored high monthly rates and receives 10–12 inbound form submissions per month because of content reach.
- Practical start: begin with short-form (TikTok easiest, then cross-post), phone-camera-only, authentic over polished; Jay grew from zero to ~400k followers and ~180k on TikTok this way.
- Followers are a status/credibility signal that warms clients before contact; the module closes with "you don't need to be perfect, you just need to begin."

# L2 detail

- `#L1-L37` — Module intro: covers why content works for AI services, what to post, why followers matter; Wk7 focus is building inbound so clients come naturally instead of cold DMs.
- `#L38-L106` — Three principles of content for AI services: (1) it is a compounding asset that works while you sleep — a post today can bring a client next month or next year, unlike outreach which needs you online; (2) AI is the most talked-about space, everyone is curious, so document and share your work and people will follow your journey; (3) content builds trust before the first conversation.
- `#L107-L216` — Trust-before-contact makes prospects warm leads vs any cold DM; Robolabs.so funnel walkthrough: they anchor a high price per month (you may copy this for your own site), the inquiry line signals scarcity ("more inbound than we can handle") which adds leverage because perception affects the value clients receive.
- `#L217-L257` — Funnel snapshot: roughly 10–12 submissions per month to that form, clients approaching them instead of the reverse; math of reach → some portion finds the site and reaches out; be a proponent of putting work out there to build a following.
- `#L258-L325` — Getting started for beginners: Jay grew from zero to almost 400,000 followers across socials at recording time; advice is to start with short form — long-form YouTube is one of the hardest to crack (needs luck or a viral hit) and is harder to produce; the three short-form platforms are YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok.
- `#L326-L440` — TikTok is the easiest: be authentic, phone camera, the algorithm finds people; RoboNuggets started on TikTok (180+ thousand followers, several videos with millions of views); oldest videos are unpolished — phone pointed at screen sharing AI nuggets, with the orange-on-a-stick pointer; no fancy equipment needed; cross-post the TikTok video to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and X because the only way to know if it works is to test.
- `#L441-L517` — There is no limit to posts per day; concrete starting move: if working on AI, Cloud Code or Antigravity, film your workspace with your phone (face not required) and share one valuable tip; authenticity and being a real person is a feature in a world of coming AI avatars; no super-polished content or fancy cameras needed — just keep posting.
- `#L518-L586` — Content is entirely free to try; the orange-on-stick videos were just quick takes kept posting until they reached an audience; volume improves technique; start at level one, post every day, believe it works, and an inflection point comes when one video reaches a wider audience and a following starts.
- `#L587-L681` — Status games: clients pay more attention if you have followers — e.g. if your practice (Nexus Labs example) shows 100,000 followers it immediately signals you know what you do; doubly true if you offer content/marketing services since you practiced what you preach; followers prove you have done the work and earned implicit trust; closing: content is leverage, works while you sleep, start now — you don't need to be perfect, you just need to begin; next module is content strategy.
