# L1 overview

- RoboLabs' one-page discovery call meeting-notes template: capture prospect info (name, company, date, call duration) and run the conversation through the six 5WH sections — Who, What, Where, When, Why, How — each with 3–4 pre-written questions and a Notes area.
- Ends with a Next Steps checklist (send follow-up email, draft proposal, schedule next call) and a follow-up date field, so every discovery call produces a concrete proposal path.

# L2 detail

- `#L1-L39` — Template header ("Use this template to capture key information during your discovery calls with prospects") plus the six 5WH question blocks, verbatim-worthy: Who — "Who is the client? What do they do?", "Who are their customers?", "Who else is involved in the decision-making?", "Who on their team would be affected by this project?"; What — "What problem are they trying to solve?", "What have they already tried?", "What does success look like for them?", "What tools or systems are they currently using?"; Where — "Where in their business is the pain point?", "Where are they losing time or money?", "Where do they see AI fitting in?"; When — "When do they need this done by?", "When did this problem start?", "When are they looking to make a decision?"; Why — "Why is this a priority right now?", "Why haven't they solved it yet?", "Why are they open to AI solutions?"; How — "How are they currently handling this process?", "How much is this problem costing them (time or money)?", "How do they prefer to work (hands-on vs hands-off)?", "How will they measure if this was worth it?" — each section has its own Notes area.
- `#L40-L45` — Next Steps checklist: send follow-up email, draft proposal, schedule next call, with a fill-in follow-up date; footer "Created by RoboLabs. Learn more at robonuggets.com".
