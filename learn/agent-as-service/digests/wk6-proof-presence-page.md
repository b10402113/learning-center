- `#L17-L25` — What You'll Learn bullets: how to capture testimonials during delivery calls, not after the fact; why attending industry events and meetups makes you the one people remember; how speaking at small stages builds credibility even without a big brand.
- `#L26-L39` — Resource 1: Event finder. Framing: "Find industry events and meetups in your area. The bar for speaking is lower than you think." Verbatim agent prompt: "I want to find AI and tech events, meetups, and conferences in my area where I can network or apply to speak. Ask me numbered questions about my location, industry, and what topics I can speak on. Then find upcoming events and give me a table with: event name, date, location, relevance to my practice, and next steps to attend or apply as a speaker."
- `#L40-L47` — Resource 2: Testimonial ask script. Framing: "Ask at the point of highest value — right after a successful delivery." Verbatim script: "Would you mind sharing a few sentences about what we worked on and how it helped? I'd love to feature it on my website." Tip: record delivery calls — when the client reacts positively, that's your testimonial; clip it and ask permission to use it later.
- `#L48-L55` — Testimonial module for your website: search for "testimonials" at https://21st.dev/community/components — each component has a built-in prompt you can send to your agent to build it into your website.
