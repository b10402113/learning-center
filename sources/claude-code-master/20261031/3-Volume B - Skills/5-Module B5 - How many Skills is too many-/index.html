Module B5 - How many Skills is too many?

How many skills is too many? The answer is simpler than you think. 📚

My rule is that as long as it does not confuse you or confuse Claude, you are fine. I show the one-prompt audit that merges and trims your library in under 30 minutes, and how to check with the context command exactly how many tokens your skills are really costing.

What you'll learn:

  • 🤔 The simple rule for too many skills, so you stop worrying and only clean up when it actually confuses

  • 🧹 The one-prompt audit, so you can spot overlaps and cut or combine skills in under 30 minutes

  • 📈 How to check your skills' token cost, so you know whether your library is denting your usage at all

    • Resources:

  • Skill audit prompt - paste into a fresh session to clean up your library:

    Audit my skills. List any that overlap, go unused, or could be merged. Tell me which to keep, cut, or combine.
