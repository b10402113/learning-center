Module C2 - Claude MD best practices

Your CLAUDE.md is the employee handbook your agent reads at the start of every session. 📕

I break down the four things a good CLAUDE.md does well: set identity, give goals, lay out rules, and map your workspace. I show my own as an example, and the simple habit of asking Claude to suggest its own updates whenever it makes sense.

What you'll learn:

  • 👤 How to set identity and goals, so your agent knows who it is, who you are, and what you are aiming for

  • 📜 Why the rules section is the real handbook, so you can tell the agent how to act and what not to touch

  • 🗺️ Why a workspace map matters, so Claude can find things instead of getting lost as your files grow
