🚀 The Operating System Mindset: ARMS Framework

🧭 Here is the mental model that makes Claude Code stronger on day 100 than it was on day one.

Every agent platform is just three parts: the model, the harness, and your context. Everyone shares the same model and harness, so your context is your edge. I show how a well-built workspace becomes your operating system, and introduce the ARMS framework we build through this masterclass.

What you'll learn:

  • ⚙️ Why the model and harness are the same for everyone, so you know your context is the only real edge you have

  • 🥇 What puts you in the top 1% of users, so you focus your effort on the one lever that actually matters

  • 🧩 The ARMS framework (applications, routines, memory, skills), so you have a simple map for building your agent
