Module G3 - Routines: 3 New Levels

🎬 Video: part 3 of 5 of the Agentic OS v2 update, above.

Routines are tasks Claude runs on a schedule, and this part covers 3 new levels for running a lot of them without drowning in output or usage.

Level 1 is active vs passive routines: an active routine hands you something to review, a passive one only pings you when something is worth flagging, like a weekly brand watch. Level 2 is capped routines: a routines tab that sorts your scheduled tasks by tokens, so you can ask Claude to trim the expensive ones to a weekly cap. Level 3 is a routine that suggests routines: every Friday it reads your sessions, spots the jobs you keep doing by hand and drops them into RUBRIC Recos for you to pick.

What you will learn:

🔕 How to make routines passive, so 50 routines aren't 50 things to review

💸 How to cap a routine's spend, so one task stops draining your usage

💡 How to let Claude spot your next routine, so your setup improves every week
