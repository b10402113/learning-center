Tip - Voice Dictation App I Use

The fastest way to prompt your agents is to stop typing and start talking. 🎙️

I show why voice prompting is roughly three times faster than typing, the free built-in options (the record button in many AI apps, plus Windows key + H for Microsoft's speech service), and the paid tool I use personally, Aqua Voice, including its custom dictionary that auto-corrects your AI terms.
