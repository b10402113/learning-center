🔳 This Week's Focus: Getting Your First Client

You've built your skills and your offer is taking shape. Now it's time to put yourself out there and start real conversations.

So, as you explore this week's lessons, you will:

Learn to see outreach as math, not rejection. Every "no" is data, and the goal is conversations, not closes

Reach out to people who already know you: friends, former colleagues, your network. This is the fastest path to your first client

Learn cold outreach: reaching out to strangers who fit your target profile. Harder than warm, but it scales beyond your network

Your first client is closer than you think. Go make it happen 🚀
