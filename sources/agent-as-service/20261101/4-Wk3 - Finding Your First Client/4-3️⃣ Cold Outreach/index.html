    Cold Outreach Message
    Cold outreach is harder than warm, but it gives you access to anyone. Lead with value, not a pitch.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to come up with your own personalized cold message:

    """
    I want to write a cold outreach email for my AI solutions practice. Ask me numbered questions about my target industry, the type of client I'm reaching, what value I can offer upfront (like a free audit, mockup, or report), and my tone preference. Then write me a cold email (under 5 sentences) and a follow-up for 3-5 days later.

    Template:
    Hey [Name],
    I ran a quick [audit type] on [their company].
    Found a few things that might help with [specific outcome].
    Want me to send over what I found?
    Let me know as it might be useful!
    """

    Terms Used

  • Cold outreach — Reaching out to people who have no idea who you are, using email or DMs

  • Deliverability — Whether your emails actually land in the inbox instead of the spam folder

  • Email warmup — A process where a tool sends and receives fake conversations to build your sender reputation before you start real outreach
