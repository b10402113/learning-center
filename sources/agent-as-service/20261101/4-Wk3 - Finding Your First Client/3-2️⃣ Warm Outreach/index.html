    Warm Outreach Message
    Warm outreach is free, fast, and has the highest conversion rate. You just need your contacts and 30 minutes a day.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to come up with your own personalized outreach message:

    """
    I want to write a warm outreach message to send to people who already know me. Ask me numbered questions about my AI practice, who I help, what I offer, and my tone preference, then write me a short outreach message (under 50 words) and a follow-up message for 3-5 days later.

    Template:
    Hey [Name],
    Quick one, I've started helping businesses automate their work with AI.
    Do you know anyone who might benefit from something like this?
    Happy to chat if you want to know more. Either way, hope you're well!

    P.S. Also have our website (website.com) if you need more info
    """
