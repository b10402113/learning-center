1️⃣ The Mindset Shift

Jay reframes how you think about outreach and rejection before diving into tactics.

The key insight: everything is just math. Track your inputs, not your outputs. The results follow.

_

What You'll Learn

Why rejection is data, not personal, and how to read it

The difference between input KPIs and output KPIs

Why targeted outreach is service, not spam

_

Terms Used

Input KPI
— Actions you control directly, like messages sent per day or calls booked per week

Output KPI
— Results you can't directly control, like revenue or clients closed
