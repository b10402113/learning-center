2️⃣ Getting Referrals

Jay covers why referrals are the highest-converting source of new clients.

Your results reports from Week 5 become the referral ammunition here.

_

What You'll Learn

Why referrals compound: one happy client leads to two or three more

Three principles: ask directly, time it after a win, make it easy

How results reports give your client specific talking points to share

_

Resources

Referral Ask Scripts

Don't wait for referrals to happen. Ask for them after a win.

Three scripts to use...

After a successful delivery:

"I'm glad this is working well. Do you know anyone else who might benefit from something similar?"

Seeding the idea casually:

"We're actually looking for ways to offer this to more people because it's been quite transformative."

Making it easy:

"If anyone comes to mind, feel free to intro us via email and I'll take it from there."
