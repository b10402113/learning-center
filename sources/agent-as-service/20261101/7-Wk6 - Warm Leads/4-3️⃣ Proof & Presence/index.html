3️⃣ Proof & Presence

Jay covers two things that quietly compound your credibility: testimonials and showing up.

_

What You'll Learn

  • How to capture testimonials during delivery calls, not after the fact

  • Why attending industry events and meetups makes you the one people remember

  • How speaking at small stages builds credibility even without a big brand

    • _

    Resources

    Event finder

    Find industry events and meetups in your area. The bar for speaking is lower than you think.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to find events:

    """
    I want to find AI and tech events, meetups, and conferences in my area where I can network or apply to speak. Ask me numbered questions about my location, industry, and what topics I can speak on. Then find upcoming events and give me a table with: event name, date, location, relevance to my practice, and next steps to attend or apply as a speaker.
    """


    Testimonial ask script

    Ask at the point of highest value — right after a successful delivery.

    "Would you mind sharing a few sentences about what we worked on and how it helped? I'd love to feature it on my website."

    Tip: Record your delivery calls. When the client reacts positively, that's your testimonial. Clip it and ask permission to use it later.


    Testimonial module you can add to your website:

    Search for "testimonials" here: https://21st.dev/community/components . Each of those have a built-in prompt you can send to your agent to build it into your website
