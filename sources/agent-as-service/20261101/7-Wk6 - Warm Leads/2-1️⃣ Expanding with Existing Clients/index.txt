1️⃣ Expanding with Existing Clients

Jay covers why going deeper with current clients is smarter than chasing new ones.

_

What You'll Learn

Why existing clients convert faster: trust, context, no pitch deck needed

How to spot adjacent problems to upsell (follow-ups, new platforms, ops)

Three retainer types: maintenance, growth, and advisory

_

Resources

Upsell Conversation Prompt

Go deeper with existing clients before chasing new ones. This prompt helps you find adjacent problems to solve.

Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to identify upsell opportunities:

"""

I have an existing client I want to expand with. Ask me numbered questions about what I've already built for them, their industry, their current pain points, and what tools they use. Then suggest 3 adjacent problems I could offer to solve, with a one-liner for each on how I'd pitch it in conversation.

"""
