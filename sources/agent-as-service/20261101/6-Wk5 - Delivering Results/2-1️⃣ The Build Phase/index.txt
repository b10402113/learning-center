1️⃣ The Build Phase

Jay covers the first phase of delivery: building the
MVP
for your client.

The goal is speed. Get something working, demo it, and get feedback before going deeper.

_

What You'll Learn

How to build an MVP by feeding your proposal to an agentic platform

Why doing a live demo achieves feedback, involvement, and clarity

The principle of under-promising and over-delivering

_

Resources

MVP Build Prompt

Start by feeding your proposal to an agentic platform and letting it guide the build.

Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to kick off the build phase:

"""

I have a client project to build. Here's the proposal I sent them:

[PASTE YOUR PROPOSAL OR DESCRIBE THE PROJECT]

Break this down into:

The core functionality — the one thing this needs to do

A step-by-step build plan starting with the simplest working version

What I can demo to the client after phase 1

Keep it simple. MVP first, bells and whistles later.

"""

Terms Used

MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
— The simplest working version of your solution, built to demo and get feedback before going deeper

Staging environment
— Your own device or system where you build and test before integrating with the client's setup
