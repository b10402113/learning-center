    Results Report Prompt
    Always show your client the return on their investment. This is what gets you retainers and referrals.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to draft a results report for a client:

    """
    I need to create a results report for a client. Ask me numbered questions about what I delivered, the metrics I tracked, and any before/after comparisons I have. Then write me a 1-page summary that includes:

    What was delivered

    Key metric (time saved, leads generated, content produced, or revenue impact)

    Dollar value of the result (use estimates if needed)

    ROI — what they paid vs what they got

    One recommendation for what to optimize or expand next

    Keep it simple enough to fit on one slide. """

    Terms Used

  • ROI (Return on Investment) — The ratio of value delivered vs what the client paid. Jay says to always quantify this with a dollar amount

  • Hypercare period — A 2-4 week window after launch where you provide free support to help the client settle into the new system
