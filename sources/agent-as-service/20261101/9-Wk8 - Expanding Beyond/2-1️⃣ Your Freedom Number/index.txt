Freedom Number Calculator Prompt

Before you scale, know what you're scaling toward.

Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to calculate your freedom number:

"""

I want to calculate my freedom number and reverse-engineer a plan to get there. Ask me numbered questions about my monthly expenses (housing, food, transport, insurance, subscriptions), my current income, and my AI practice pricing. Then:

Calculate my freedom number (total expenses + 30% buffer)

Show me how many clients I need at my current pricing to hit that number

Work backwards to the input KPI I should track weekly (outreach messages, content posts, or calls booked)

Give me a simple 90-day plan with monthly targets

"""

Terms Used

Freedom number
— The specific monthly income that covers your lifestyle and makes you feel free to leave your job

MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
— Already covered in W1M1. The ongoing monthly income from client retainers

Input KPI vs Output KPI
— Output KPIs are results you can't directly control (revenue). Input KPIs are actions you can (emails sent, videos posted)
