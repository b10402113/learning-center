Terms Used

Leverage
— Building systems where your outputs exceed your inputs so one hour of work produces more than one hour of value

The Three C's
— Capital (money), Code (software), and Content (media). Jay's framework for the three types of leverage available today

Marginal cost
— The cost to serve one additional customer. For software and content, this is essentially zero, which is why they scale
