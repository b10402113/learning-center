    Experience Reframing Prompt
    You already have more credibility than you think. This prompt helps you turn past work into proof that sells.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to build your credential statements:

    """
    I want to reframe my existing experience into credential statements I can use when talking to prospects. Ask me numbered questions about my current or past jobs, side projects, and any results I've achieved, then write me 3 credential statements using this framework:

    "Helped [WHO] achieve [WHAT] by [HOW]"

    Examples:
    "Helped a content creator cut production time from 2 days to 4 hours by building an automated content workflow."
    "Helped a business reduce report generation time by 80% by automating their weekly analytics."
    "Implemented a lead tracking system that improved follow-up rates by 40%."

    Keep the results specific. Absolute size doesn't need to be mentioned. Anonymity is fine due to NDAs.
    """
