    LinkedIn Announcement Prompt
    Tell your network you've started an AI practice. This is how referrals begin.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to come up with your own personalized post:

    """
    I want to write a LinkedIn post announcing that I've started an AI solutions practice. Ask me numbered questions about my background, my practice name, what I offer, and which of the 3 styles below I prefer, then write me a final post that feels like mine and doesn't look exactly like the template.

    Style 1 — Simple announcement:
    I have some personal news to share — I've started an AI solutions practice called [PRACTICE NAME].
    We help [WHO YOU HELP] with [WHAT YOU DO].
    If you or anyone in your network could use help with AI, I'd love to chat.
    Check it out: [YOUR URL]

    Style 2 — Story-driven:
    For the past few months, I've been [WHAT YOU'VE BEEN DOING WITH AI].
    That journey led me to start [PRACTICE NAME], an AI solutions practice that helps [WHO YOU HELP] with [WHAT YOU DO].
    If this sounds like something someone in your network could use, I'd love an intro.
    [YOUR URL]

    Style 3 — Casual:
    Trying something new — I've started a little AI practice on the side called [PRACTICE NAME].
    Helping [WHO YOU HELP] with [WHAT YOU DO].
    Still early days but it's been really interesting so far.
    If you know anyone who might benefit, happy to chat.
    """
