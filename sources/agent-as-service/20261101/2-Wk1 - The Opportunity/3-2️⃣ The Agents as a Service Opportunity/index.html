    One-Liner Positioning Template
    You need to be able to explain what you do in one line. This is what you'll use in DMs, at events, and on calls.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to come up with your own one-liner:

    """
    I want to craft a one-liner for my AI solutions practice. Ask me numbered questions so I can give you my answers, then write me 3 options for a one-liner that I can use to introduce myself and my practice in 5 seconds.

    Formula:
    [YOUR NAME] + [PRACTICE NAME] + [WHAT YOU ARE] + [WHO YOU HELP]

    Example:
    "I'm working on Robo Labs, an AI solutions practice that helps brands and content businesses use AI in meaningful ways."
    """

    Terms Used

  • MSP (Managed Service Provider) — Companies that manage IT systems for businesses. The 80s/90s model Jay compares to today's AI agent opportunity

  • Commoditization — When a service becomes so common and easy that anyone can do it, removing your competitive edge
