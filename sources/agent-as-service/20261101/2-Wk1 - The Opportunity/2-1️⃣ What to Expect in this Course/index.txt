Terms Used

MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
— The amount of money you earn every month from ongoing client contracts, not one-off projects

Transition Curve
— A model from the 1970s that maps the emotional stages of any big change: uninformed optimism, informed pessimism, valley of despair, and informed optimism
