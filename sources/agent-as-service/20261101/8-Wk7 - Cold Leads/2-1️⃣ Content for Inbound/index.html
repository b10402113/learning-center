1️⃣ Content for Inbound

Jay covers why content is the most leveraged way to attract clients without cold outreach.

Content is a compounding asset that works while you sleep.

_

What You'll Learn

  • Why content builds trust before the first conversation even happens

  • Why short-form (TikTok first) is the best starting point for beginners

  • How followers signal credibility and directly impact client trust

    Content Starter Prompt
    You don't need fancy equipment. Just your phone and something valuable to say.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to come up with your first content ideas:

    """
    I want to start posting short-form content about AI on TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts. Ask me numbered questions about my AI practice, what tools I use, what I've built recently, and who my audience is. Then give me 5 specific video ideas I can record today using just my phone pointed at my screen. Each idea should have a hook (first sentence) and a one-line description of what I'd show.
    """

    _

    Terms Used

  • Inbound — Clients who come to you because they found your content, instead of you reaching out to them

  • Short-form — Videos under 60 seconds posted on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts
