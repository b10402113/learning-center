2️⃣ Content Strategy

Jay dives into what content actually works: hooks, consistency, email capture, and posting volume.

_

What You'll Learn

Why a great hook on mediocre content outperforms great content with a weak hook

How to batch content and post 2-3 times per day on short-form platforms

Why your email list is the only audience you truly own

_

Resources

Hook writing prompt

If you master one thing in content, make it hooks. A great hook on mediocre content outperforms great content with a weak hook.

Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to write hooks for your videos:

"""

I want to write hooks for my short-form AI content. Ask me numbered questions about the topic I'm covering and my audience. Then write me 5 hook options for each video idea. Each hook should be one sentence that creates curiosity or makes a bold claim. Also suggest a visual hook (something on screen that grabs attention in the first second).

"""

Terms Used

Hook
— The first sentence, visual, or headline in a video that grabs attention and keeps people watching

Rented space
— Your followers on social platforms that can be taken away if your account gets banned, unlike an email list you own
