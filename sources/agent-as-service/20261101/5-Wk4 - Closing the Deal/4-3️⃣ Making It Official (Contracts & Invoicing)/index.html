    Contract Template Prompt
    Every client engagement needs a written contract. This prompt helps you draft one.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to draft a contract for a specific client:

    """
    I need to draft a simple contract for an AI agent project. Ask me numbered questions about the client, the scope of work, timeline, pricing, number of revision rounds, and who owns the final work. Then write me a clear, plain-language contract that covers these 6 things:

    Scope — what exactly I'm delivering

    Timeline — when I'll deliver it

    Payment terms — how much, by when, and how

    Revisions — how many rounds of changes are included

    IP ownership — who owns the work when it's done

    Termination — how either party can end the agreement

    Keep it simple. No legal jargon. """


    Business structure research prompt

    If you're unsure whether to start as a sole proprietor or incorporate:

    """
    I'm starting an AI solutions practice in [YOUR COUNTRY/REGION]. I'm currently [employed / freelancing / between jobs]. Ask me numbered questions about my situation, then explain the simplest way to start legally, when I should consider incorporating, and what the tax implications are for my location.
    """

    Terms Used

  • Scope creep — When the client keeps adding work beyond the original agreement because boundaries weren't set

  • LLC (Limited Liability Company) — A business structure that separates your personal assets from business liability

  • Sole proprietor — Operating your business as yourself with no formal company setup, the simplest way to start
