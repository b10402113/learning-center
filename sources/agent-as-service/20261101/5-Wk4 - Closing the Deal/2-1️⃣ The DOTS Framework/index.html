    Discovery Call Prep Prompt
    Your role in a discovery call is to ask questions and listen, not pitch. This prompt helps you prepare.

    Tip: Send this prompt to your agent to prepare for a specific discovery call:

    """
    I have a discovery call coming up with a potential client. Ask me numbered questions about their industry, what I know about their business so far, and what I think their problem might be, then write me a list of 5WH questions (what, who, where, when, why, how) I can ask during the call.

    Also give me a simple meeting notes template I can fill in during the call.
    """
