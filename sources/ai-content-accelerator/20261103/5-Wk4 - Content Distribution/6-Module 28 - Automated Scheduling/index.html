Module 28 - Automated Scheduling

Posting by hand means uploading each piece and pasting its caption, one app at a time.

A scheduler takes that job off you: one video goes out to all your accounts, at times you pick.

I use Blotato, built by Sabrina, a longtime friend of our community, and your agent can run it.

    How /post Works

    /post is the skill I use to schedule. Grab the baseline version from Jay's Resources to tweak.

    It saves your preferences: your posting times, your accounts and where each post should go.

    By default it connects to Blotato, and your agent does the scheduling there for you.

    ⚙️ Set it up once: you need a Blotato account, your API key (it's in Blotato's settings), and each account connected once.

    Connecting an account takes you to that app's sign-in page, and at the end you get an account ID. Give it to your agent.

    With the account IDs and your API key, your agent can post for you, and you barely need Blotato's own screens again.

    Prompts to Send Your Agent

    Get /post: download it from Jay's Resources and send your agent the install prompt on that page.

    Set up Blotato and /post: it's a one-time job, so send this first:

    """Set up Blotato with my /post skill, so you can schedule my posts for me. If I haven't installed /post from RoboNuggets yet, tell me and stop there.

    1. Ask me, one at a time: which accounts I want you to post to (for example Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Threads), and whether I have a Blotato account yet. If I don't, send me to blotato.com/?ref=robonuggets (RoboNuggets members get 30% off all plans), and check the plan I pick includes API access, because that's how you post for me.

    2. Show me where my API key is in Blotato's settings. Make a private secrets file for it and ask me to paste the key there myself, never into our chat or into the skill. Keep that file out of anything I share or upload.

    3. Walk me through connecting each account in Blotato, one at a time. Each one gives me an account ID: ask me for it and save it in /post. If an account is brand new, remind me to warm it up first and leave it out for now.

    4. Check the key works, list the accounts Blotato sees, and confirm with me which ones are mine. Only ever post to those.

    5. Ask me my time zone and my posting times (the default is 9am, 1pm and 5pm), and save them in /post.

    6. Do a dry run with one of my videos and show me exactly what would go to each app. Nothing goes live in this step."""

    Schedule a week of clips: paste your Render all instructions from RUBRIC Editor, then add this line:

    """[paste the Render all instructions from RUBRIC Editor here, or the paths to my finished videos]

    /post these clips one per day from today until 7 days from now, across my usual channels for shorts: Instagram, YouTube Shorts and TikTok."""

    Pick your posting times: to set times that fit your day, send this:

    """Help me pick my posting times for /post.

    1. Ask me my time zone, when I'm usually awake and can check my phone, and how many posts a day I can keep up (once a day is fine, five is fine too).

    2. Suggest times while I'm awake, so I can fix or delete a post fast if something goes wrong, with one line on why.

    3. Save them as the defaults in my /post skill, and show me what changed."""
