Module 26 - Why Post Everywhere

Before the scheduling lessons, here are a few principles for how to think about posting.

Each app's algorithm keeps changing, because a big team works on it every single day.

So instead of chasing it, think from first principles about what every app wants.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🧠 Why apps want useful videos: they compete with Netflix and Spotify for your time

  • 🔺 The Content Pyramid's third tier: volume, then improve, and now quality on top

  • 🌍 Why you post every piece on every channel, even at zero followers

    • _

    Quality Means Useful

    Put yourself in the shoes of the team at YouTube or Instagram.

    They compete for your time with every other app, like Netflix, Prime Video and Spotify.

    So they tune the app to show what people watch, and you watch a video in full when it's useful.

    🔺 The Content Pyramid, complete: volume at the base, then improve with every post you make.

    Now that you've done some reps, the third tier sits on top: quality, which really means useful.

    I still work this way: lots of reps, improving every time, with quality as the North Star.

    🎯 What quality is made of: the Content Trifecta from Week 1.

    The biggest circle is what others find useful, because that's what earns watch time.

    Blend it with what you enjoy and, as much as you can, what only you can offer. That's the sweet spot.

    🚀 Recommended Action: post your next piece on every short-form app you have, even if you're starting from zero.
