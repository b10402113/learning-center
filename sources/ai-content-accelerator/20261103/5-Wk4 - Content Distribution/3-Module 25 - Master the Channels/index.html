Module 25 - Master the Channels

Before you spread your content around, let's look at where it can actually go.

There are just three big kinds of content: shorts, longs and text.

Try them all if you can. But if you can only start with one, make it shorts.

They get you the most reps and the fastest feedback, even at zero followers.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 📱 Shorts: TikTok first for the fastest feedback, then Instagram, then YouTube Shorts

  • 🎬 Longs: YouTube builds the most trust, but each video takes far longer to make

  • ✍️ Text: X, LinkedIn posts and newsletters, and why I wouldn't start there

    • _

    ⏳ One long video can equal about 60 shorts: say a viewer watches your long video for 10 minutes, and each short for 10 seconds.

    They'd need about 60 of your shorts to spend the same time with you, which is why YouTube builds the most trust.

    It's a rough example, and the real number can be lower or higher.

    ✍️ Why not text first: people scrolling X mostly don't have buyer intent, at least far less than on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok.

    And a newsletter has no algorithm to help new people find you. Most people find a newsletter through someone's videos first.

    That's what I learned when I tried to start RoboNuggets as a newsletter.

    💬 Speak the same language: other creators, sponsors and partners think in shorts, longs and text too, so it helps when you meet them.

    _

    🚀 Recommended Action: start with shorts, and see if you can post 5 of them this week.

    At this stage it's fine to ship what you like, as long as it's useful and truthful. That's how you learn what works in your niche.
