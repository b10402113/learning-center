Module 27 - Reminder

Before you hand your posting to a scheduler, make sure the account is warmed up.

A new account that only posts through software doesn't look like a person using the app.

_

👋 Use a new account like a person first: log in, post, watch, like and comment in your niche.

⏳ Brand new? Post by hand this week and connect it later. Had it a while? Connect it now.

_

💡 Start with one: when you connect an account, schedule one post first.

Check it goes live before you schedule the rest.
