Module 22 - Automated Editing

Edit a few pieces by hand first, so you can tell when your agent gets a cut wrong.

Then hand the cutting to /cut and review it in RUBRIC Editor, in a loop I call Reinforcement Learning.

_

What You'll Learn

  • ✂️ How /cut works: a transcript with the time of every word, then the last clean take of every line

  • 🔄 Review in RUBRIC Editor, trim and comment, Teach my /cut, then Export and your agent renders it

  • 🎞️ Prefer your own editor? Connect Premiere Pro with an MCP, or run /search-connectors

    • _

    Get RUBRIC Editor

    Download RUBRIC Editor and copy its install prompt, then send it to your agent. Like RUBRIC Scripts, it runs on your own computer and opens in your browser.

    It ships with the /cut skill: give it your raw recording, and your agent cuts it and puts it on RUBRIC Editor for you to review.

    🧠 How /cut knows what to cut: your agent transcribes the recording with the time of every word, so it knows what you said and when.

    By default it keeps the last clean take of every line, and every fix you make in RUBRIC Editor teaches it how you talk.

    _

    🚀 Recommended Action: edit a few of this week's pieces by hand, in the app you post to or in CapCut.

    Then hand over what repeats, and keep the calls that need you, like which take sounds most like you.

    🎬 Want animations? /animate adds them on top of your cut. It's optional and more advanced, so skip it for now if you like.

    The bonus page after this lesson links my lessons on it.

    Prompts to Send Your Agent

    Cut a recording with /cut: give it the path to your raw recording. A line of context helps it get it right the first time, like this:

    """/cut [path to my raw recording, for example C:/Videos/my-shorts.mkv]

    this is one long take of me reading [7] short videos from my teleprompter, one after another: [the titles, one per line]. cut each one into its own clean vertical clip and put them all on one RUBRIC Editor so i can review them."""

    Or let /cut ask you everything: if you'd rather go step by step, send this:

    """/cut my recording. go one step at a time and ask me one question at a time.

    - first ask me where the raw recording is, then what it is (a short, a long video or a lesson)

    - transcribe it, keep the last clean take of every line, and cut the pauses and false starts

    - do a dry run first and show me the kept text before you render anything

    - then put the cut on RUBRIC Editor so i can listen to it and trim it

    - when i send my notes back, fix the cut and write down what i liked and didn't like, so the next cut starts closer"""

    Pick your transcription service: AssemblyAI is the default. To switch, or to connect your key, send this:

    """Set up the transcription for my /cut skill. Ask me one question at a time.

    1. Ask me which one I want: AssemblyAI (the default, fast and paid per hour), Whisper from OpenAI (free, runs on my computer, much slower) or ElevenLabs Scribe (fast, a bit pricier, good if I already pay for ElevenLabs).

    2. If it needs an API key, open the right page for me, tell me where to click to make the key, and show me the safest way to give it to you. Never paste my key into a chat log or a file I share.

    3. Change /cut to use it, and run a quick test on a short clip to check the words come back with their times.

    4. Tell me in plain words how long an hour of video will take and roughly what it costs."""

    Export your cut: once you're happy with it in RUBRIC Editor, send this:

    """export my approved cut at 1080p. ask me one question at a time.

    - first ask me which project, then whether you should render this clip or all of them

    - render from the cut i approved, with every trim i made in RUBRIC Editor

    - when it's done, tell me where the file is and check the sound plays all the way through"""

    Connect Premiere Pro: if you edit long videos in Premiere Pro, send this to set up the connector I use:

    """Connect my Premiere Pro so you can work in it with me, using the Adobe Premiere Pro MCP: github.com/hetpatel-11/adobe_premiere_pro_mcp

    1. Read its README first, and tell me in plain words what it can do and what it needs.

    2. Walk me through the install one step at a time, and wait for me at each step.

    3. Test it: open my project, list my sequences, and tell me what you see.

    4. Wait for my go before you change anything in my projects."""

    Find a connector for your editor: for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or anything else, grab /search-connectors from Jay's Resources, then send this:

    """/search-connectors for my video editor. first ask me which editor i use (premiere pro, davinci resolve, final cut pro or something else).

    - look for official connectors first, then community ones, and check each one is still kept up

    - tell me the best one and how to install it, in plain words

    - wait for my go before you install anything"""
