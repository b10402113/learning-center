Module 23 - Which Account to Use

As you get ready for your first post, pick the account it all goes out from.

My take: a separate account, and before you post from a new one, do some warming up.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🧭 Why I use a separate account: it keeps your personal life apart from your work

  • 🌡️ Separate doesn't mean new: reuse an old account, or warm up a new one for about a week

  • 🔍 Why you post by hand first, and how your new feed turns into a research feed

    • _

    🚀 Recommended Action: pick one account for this week's 5 pieces, and post them by hand, straight from the app.

    If it's a new account, warm it up first: watch, follow, like and comment like a normal person.

    🌡️ How long to warm up: most guides say about a week, and up to 2 weeks if you want to be careful. Wait about 4 weeks on a new account before you automate posting.

    These come from other creators' experience, not a rule TikTok or Instagram publishes. If you keep getting fewer than 10 views a post, the app has likely tagged you as a bot, so warm up another account or start a new one.
