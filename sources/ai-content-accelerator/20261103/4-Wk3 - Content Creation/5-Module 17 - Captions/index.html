Module 17 - Captions

Captions aren't the most important part of your post, but they do help people find you.

Write them for AEO, answer engine optimisation, so AI answers can quote you.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🖼️ Why the caption matters most on a photo or carousel, where there's nothing to hear

  • 🔎 Write lines that make sense on their own, name the thing, and use real numbers

  • 🏷️ End with Questions we answered, and keep it to a few relevant hashtags

    • _

    💡 Hand it to your agent: the content is the meat, and a lot of people don't even read the caption.

    As long as it's AEO optimised, related to the post and not lying, that's completely okay.

    Later this week, RUBRIC Scripts writes a caption like this with every script, ready for you to check.

    🔎 Name the tool, don't repeat the hook: your hook keeps things vague to make people curious, but your caption should say exactly what you're talking about, like Claude Code, so search and AI answers can find you.
