Module 19 - Operating System

Before the two apps, let's zoom out and look at the whole content OS we're building together.

It's the same one I use every day to run our brand: a set of skills, software and principles for content that drives your business.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🧭 The simple view: your content identity, ideas, creation and posting, in one loop

  • 🤖 Why your agent sits in the centre, doing the work a whole content team used to do

  • 🧩 The detailed view: every skill and app in the OS, and the week you learn each one

    • _

    The Simple View

    Week 1, your content identity: the core principles, plus two frameworks. The Content Trifecta is what to make, and the Content Pyramid is how to make it. Revisit Week 1.

    Week 2, ideation: ideas from the outliers in your industry with /outliers, the latest news with /latest, and your own ideas, which are a really good source too. Revisit Week 2.

    Week 3, creation: your /hooks skill and your own /tone skill, then two apps we use every day: RUBRIC Scripts and RUBRIC Editor.

    Week 4, posting: next week we automate posting and distribution, and that closes the loop.

    💡 Your agent is in the centre of all of it. What used to take a whole content team, you now do with your agent and yourself.

    And the more you improve your skills, the better every step gets.

    _

    The Detailed View

    Skills sit on the left of each step, and the software on the right. You check each step until it runs on its own.

    We'll come back to this picture near the end of the course.
