Module 15 - Hooks

In the first few seconds, people decide whether to stay or scroll.

That's the job of your hook, bar none the most important part of your script.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🎯 Why a video is about 50% packaging and 50% content, and why we start with shorts

  • 🧩 The 9 hook shapes, each with a real post from AI and one from outside it

  • 🔖 The visual hook, and how to build your own hook library in one saved folder

    • _

    The 9 Hook Shapes

  • The contrarian: say the opposite of what your audience has been told

  • The investigator: dig into something and show what you found

  • The teacher: promise a clear lesson they'll walk away with

  • The experimenter: test something yourself and share the result

  • The fortuneteller: say what's coming next

  • The magician: show a result so good it looks impossible

  • The curiosity gap: tease something they need to know, then make them wait

  • The versus: put two things head to head

  • The list: promise a set number of things

    • _

    🚀 Recommended Action: say every hook out loud, and cut any word you stumble on.

    Keep it true, because a hook is a promise the rest of your content has to keep.

    Later this week, RUBRIC Scripts uses it on its own to give you 3 hooks for every script.

    Prompts to Send Your Agent

    Suggest hooks for an idea: have an idea from last week ready, then send this:

    """Suggest hooks for my idea with /hooks. My idea: [one line, plus the link it came from if I have one]

    1. Read my /hooks skill first. If it isn't installed, tell me and stop there.

    2. If you can't tell from my idea, ask me who it's for and what they get by the end, one at a time.

    3. Give me 3 hooks in 3 different shapes, each short enough to say out loud in about 3 seconds, with one line on why that shape fits.

    4. Write them the way I talk, with my /tone skill if I have one.

    5. Let me pick, mix or rewrite one, and remember what I picked so your next suggestions get closer."""

    Find the hook for your script: /hooks names the 9 shapes and suggests the one that fits, so send this:

    """Find the right hook for my script with /hooks. Here is my script: [paste it here]

    1. Read my /hooks skill first. If it isn't installed, tell me and stop there.

    2. Ask me these one at a time and wait for each answer: who this is for, and the one real result or proof the video delivers (never make one up).

    3. Name the 9 hook shapes, then tell me which one fits my script best and why, in one line.

    4. Write 3 hooks in that shape, short enough to say out loud in about 3 seconds, and tell me what my first frame should show.

    5. Let me pick one, then put it at the top of my script."""

    Save a hook you liked: when a post stops your scroll, bookmark it, then send this:

    """Add this hook to my /hooks library: [paste the link, or describe the post]

    1. Look at the first few seconds and write down the hook word for word, with the creator and the link. If you can't open it, tell me and I'll paste the words.

    2. Tell me which of the 9 shapes it is. If it's a new shape, give it a short name and a one-line definition.

    3. Add it to my /hooks skill as an example (or as a new shape), so you can suggest it next time.

    4. Tell me in one line how I could use this shape for my own topic."""
