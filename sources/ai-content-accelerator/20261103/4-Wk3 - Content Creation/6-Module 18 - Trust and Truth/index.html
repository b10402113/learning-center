Module 18 - Trust and Truth

One of the best things you can do as a creator is under-promise and over-deliver.

Your hook, title and thumbnail are a promise, so promise what you can beat.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🎣 Why clickbait costs you your reputation, and how YouTube spots it in your numbers

  • ⏳ When a viral post's promise expires, and why I seldom use "this is insane" thumbnails

  • 🤝 Why trust compounds in education, and how your face and honesty about AI build it

    • _

    💡 Before you post: whether you wrote the hook first or last, recheck that it still matches the content you ended up making.

    It's about 50% the hook and 50% what you actually say, so if both are still true, you're good to ship.

    🙂 Faceless is completely fine to start. But if you're playing the long game, showing your face builds trust faster, and talking to a camera is a skill anyone can learn.
