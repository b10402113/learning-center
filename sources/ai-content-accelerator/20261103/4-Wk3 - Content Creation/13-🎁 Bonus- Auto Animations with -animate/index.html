🎁 Bonus: Auto Animations with /animate

If you want animations in your videos, like the ones in my recent videos, that's what /animate is for.

After your cut is sequenced, it lets your agent add the animations for you, built from a library of ready-made elements.

It's optional and more advanced, so you don't need to start here. Come back once your cuts feel right.

_

Get the Skill

/animate: grab it from j9 - /animate Skill in Jay's Resources. It comes with its install prompt.

Once it's in, turn on Teach my /animate in RUBRIC Editor, and your comments on an animated clip teach /animate what you like.
