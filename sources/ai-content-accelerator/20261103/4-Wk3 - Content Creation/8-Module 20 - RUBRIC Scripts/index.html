Module 20 - RUBRIC Scripts

Last week you found your ideas with /outliers, /latest and your own experience.

Now RUBRIC Scripts turns them into scripts that are ready to record, all in one place instead of lost in a chat window.

It's part of RUBRIC, the custom software we build for the RoboNuggets community, and it's made to work alongside your agent.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 📋 Type /rubricscripts with your ideas, and your agent writes the scripts with your /hooks and /tone

  • ✍️ Pick from 3 hooks, highlight to comment, and Teach my /tone so every script sounds more like you

  • 📱 Read your scripts on Telegram, on your computer or on a teleprompter, and record them all in one go

    • _

    Get RUBRIC Scripts

    Download RUBRIC Scripts and copy its install prompt, then send it to your agent.

    It opens in your browser on localhost, which just means it runs on your own computer, and it comes with the /rubricscripts skill.

    By default it looks for your /hooks and /tone skills, and teaches you how to make them if you don't have them yet.

    💡 Video and channels come in Week 4: the video window and the TikTok, Instagram, Threads and YouTube Shorts row are a preview of your video before you post, and we cover them next week.

    _

    Prompts to Send Your Agent

    Turn your ideas into scripts: type /rubricscripts with your ideas, as detailed or as short as you like:

    """/rubricscripts turn these ideas into scripts for short videos:

    1. [idea one, plus the link it came from if I have one]

    2. [idea two]

    3. [idea three]

    Use my /hooks and /tone skills, give me 3 hooks for each script, write a caption for each one, and add the links you used as references. Never make up a fact, a number or a link. When they're in RUBRIC Scripts, give me the link to open it."""

    Load your ideas without writing yet: if you'd rather pick from your list first, send this:

    """Load my content ideas into RUBRIC Scripts, so I can turn them into scripts.

    1. Run /rubricscripts first, so you know how RUBRIC Scripts works. If it isn't installed, tell me and stop there.

    2. Find my shortlist: my latest /outliers and /latest results, and any ideas I saved myself. Show them to me as a short numbered list.

    3. Ask me which ones to load, and wait for my answer. If I don't have a shortlist yet, offer to run /latest and /outliers again first.

    4. Write a script for each idea I pick, with 3 hooks to choose from, and load them into RUBRIC Scripts.

    5. Tell me how many scripts are ready and give me the link to open RUBRIC Scripts."""

    Tip: talk your comments instead of typing them, with a voice tool like Aqua Voice or Wispr Flow.

    Make RUBRIC Scripts your own: it's all malleable, so ask your agent for what you want, like this:

    """Change my RUBRIC Scripts: [for example, give me 4 hooks instead of 3, make every caption shorter, or remove the windows I don't use].

    1. Run /rubricscripts first so you know how the app is built.

    2. Tell me in plain words what you'll change, and wait for my go.

    3. Make the change, keep all my scripts and comments as they are, and give me the link to check it."""

    Read them on your phone: to get your finished scripts on Telegram, send this:

    """Send me my finished scripts on Telegram, so I can read them on my phone.

    1. Check if you can already send me Telegram messages. If you're Claude Code, the official way is the Telegram channel plugin: code.claude.com/docs/en/channels. If you can't yet, walk me through the setup one step at a time, and wait for me at each step.

    2. Ask me which scripts are ready: the ones I picked a hook for in RUBRIC Scripts, or the ones I name.

    3. Send each script as its own message: the title on top, then the script in short paragraphs, nothing else.

    4. Tell me how many you sent."""

    Read them on your computer: for a clean page to read from, send this:

    """Make me a simple page with my finished scripts from RUBRIC Scripts, so I can read them while I record.

    1. Ask me which scripts to include, or take every one I picked a hook for.

    2. Put them on one page, one after another, each with its title, the hook I picked, then the script in short lines with big, easy-to-read text.

    3. Open it for me, or give me the link."""

    Send them to a teleprompter: I use my own simple teleprompter, with a desktop and a phone version. Grab it from Jay's Resources, then send this:

    """Send my finished scripts from RUBRIC Scripts to my teleprompter, all on one long page, so I can record them in one take.

    1. Check my teleprompter is installed and running. If it isn't, tell me and help me set it up.

    2. Put the scripts in order, each one starting with its title and a short pause line, so I can tell where one ends and the next starts.

    3. Give me the links to open it on my computer and on my phone."""

    🎬 Record them all in one go: load all your scripts on one long page and read them one after another in one continuous take.

    Set aside an hour on your content day and you can record 10 to 15 shorts. Your agent cuts them into separate videos in the Automated Editing lesson.
