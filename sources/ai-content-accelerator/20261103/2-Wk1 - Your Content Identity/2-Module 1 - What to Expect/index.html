Module 1 - What to Expect

Here's what to expect: an action-oriented 30-day boot camp, in 4 weekly modules.

  • Week 1, Your Content Identity: you go from consuming content to producing it

  • Week 2, Ideation: a steady flow of content ideas, with AI doing the research

  • Week 3, Creation: quality content, made faster with AI
