Module 12 - Finding Outliers

An outlier doesn't need millions of views. It's a post that beats its own normal.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 📏 An outlier is relative: 1M views on a channel that usually gets 500K is 2x

  • 🔍 You don't need big numbers: a small channel's video at almost 8x its normal counts

  • 🤖 vidIQ shows an outlier score as you browse, or /outliers does the maths for you

    • _

    Grab the Skills

    /outliers: finds the posts in your niche that beat their own normal, on YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, X, Instagram and LinkedIn, and ranks them.

    Find the skill zip at the bottom of the page.

    ⚙️ How it works: type /outliers and your agent divides each post's views by its account's normal views, then ranks the biggest outliers on a page you can sort and shortlist.

    💡 Tips:

    1. Too few results? Go to a bigger niche or a broader topic. Or cast a wider net: run /latest over 14 or 28 days, or add more peer accounts to /outliers.

    2. Be creative with topics, not just your industry or job. Go from broad to narrow: AI, then local AI. Claude in general, then Claude for 3D work in Blender.

    3. Teach it your taste. Add the ideas you like to the cart, press Copy for my agent and paste it back to your agent. It saves what you liked to the skill's taste file and uses it to rank and shape your next run.
