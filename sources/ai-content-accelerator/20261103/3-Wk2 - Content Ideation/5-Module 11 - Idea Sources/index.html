Module 11 - Idea Sources

A lot of good ideas come from your own experience. Agents can help you find even more.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🔎 Find the well of knowledge for your niche: where the latest news lands first

  • 🐦 Real-time sources like X, GitHub and official docs beat TikTok, Instagram and YouTube

  • ⚡ Try /latest for the last 7 days in your niche, or set it to 14 or 28 days

    • 🚀 Recommended Action: run /latest for your niche and pick one idea.

    Point your phone at your screen and talk about it. If you have the time, post it today to get the reps in.

    _

    Grab the Skill

    /latest checks the last 7 days across social media, Reddit and the web for fresh ideas.

    Find the skill zip at the bottom of the page.

    ⚙️ How it works: type /latest, and in 2 to 3 minutes you get up to 10 ranked content ideas, each with an angle and a source link, on a page you can search and filter.

    ℹ️ Don't know how to use Skills? Watch this: Skills Explained.
    ℹ️ Don't have an agent set up yet? Watch A1 and A2 to get started with Claude.
    🎁 A more detailed breakdown of this skill is in the next bonus module

    To install it, send this prompt to your agent:

    """I'm giving you a resource called Latest. It's in the file robonuggets-latest.zip (in my Downloads folder unless I say otherwise).

    Follow the README to set it up.
    Recommend how it would best apply to our setup in plain language,
    and ask me a few questions to clarify my intent before you install it."""

    After setup: type /latest, or name your niche, like /latest home gym equipment. Want a longer window? Say "last 14 days" or "last 28 days".

    💡 Tips:

    1. Too few results? Go to a bigger niche or a broader topic. Or cast a wider net: run /latest over 14 or 28 days, or add more peer accounts to /outliers.

    2. Be creative with topics, not just your industry or job. Go from broad to narrow: AI, then local AI. Claude in general, then Claude for 3D work in Blender.

    3. Teach it your taste. Add the ideas you like to the cart, press Copy for my agent and paste it back to your agent. It saves what you liked to the skill's taste file and uses it to rank and shape your next run.

    _

    Other Useful Prompts to Send Your Agent

    Get alerts: if your agent can send you alerts (Hermes, OpenClaw, ChatGPT), send this:

    """Set up a news alert for me about [my niche, for example AI agents].

    1. First, list the sources where news in this niche shows up first: official company blogs, docs and changelogs, the main companies' and people's accounts on X, GitHub, and the subreddits or forums where it gets discussed. Show me the list and let me add or remove sources before you start.

    2. Check those sources [twice a day, at 8am and 5pm my time] and send me an alert each time.

    3. Each alert has the 3 to 5 most important new things since the last alert. For each one: what happened in one line, why it matters to someone making content in my niche, and the source link. Skip anything you already sent me.

    4. End each alert with one content idea I could post today from it.

    5. If nothing important happened, send one line saying so instead of padding it.

    Ask me any questions you need before you set it up, then confirm the schedule back to me."""

    Find your sources: not sure where your niche's news comes from? Send this to research it.

    """I make content about [my niche] for [who I make it for]. Help me find my well of knowledge: the places where news and new ideas in this niche show up first, before they reach TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

    1. Ask me 2 or 3 questions about my niche and audience first if you need to.

    2. Research and list the best sources in four groups: official sources (company sites, docs, changelogs), the people and accounts who post first (on X or wherever my niche talks), communities (subreddits, forums, groups), and tools or data (for example trending lists, launch sites, industry newsletters).

    3. For each source give me the link, why it is early, and how often it posts.

    4. Mark the 5 I should check every week, and tell me which ones you could check for me on a schedule."""
