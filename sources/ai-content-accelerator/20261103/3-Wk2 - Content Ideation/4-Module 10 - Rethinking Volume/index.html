Module 10 - Rethinking Volume

How much should you post? If you're just beginning, as many as you humanly can.

The Content Pyramid: volume at the base, then improve or try something new every post.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🔁 Post as much as you can. Platforms don't cap you, and my rule was 3 a day

  • 📱 Edit and post by hand from your phone at first, before you automate anything

  • 📈 Volume forces you to iterate. Early on, try big changes like a new channel or format

    • 🚀 Recommended Action: set a goal to ship your first 100 pieces of content.

    Draw 100 checkboxes in a notebook or on a board, and tick one off every time you post.
