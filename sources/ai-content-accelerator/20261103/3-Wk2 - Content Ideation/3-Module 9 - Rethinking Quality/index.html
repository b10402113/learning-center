Module 9 - Rethinking Quality

The biggest thing stopping people from starting is thinking they need quality first. You don't.

_

What You'll Learn

  • 🌱 Your first posts can be rough, even faceless. Chris's, MrBeast's and mine were too

  • 📊 Quality has thresholds: worry about it more once you reach 80 to 100K followers

  • 🔊 Quality means useful, not fancy production. Just make sure people can hear you

    • 🚀 Recommended Action: record one simple video today about something you find useful.

    Post it to Instagram and TikTok by hand. Rather start with text? Post it on LinkedIn.
